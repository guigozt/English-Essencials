? + verb    |   Subject     |   Answer

Who's       |     Tom?      |   He's a friend from work
What's      |   Your name?  |   My name's Guilherme
Where are   |   you from?   |   I'm from Brazil
When's      |  the concert? |   It's on Wednesday
How are     |     you?      |   I'm fine
How old     |   are you?    |   I'm twenty-two years old

Useful:

    I'm fine... See you later ->    Vejo você depois
    I'm fine... I'm late for school ->  Eu estou atrasado para a escola
