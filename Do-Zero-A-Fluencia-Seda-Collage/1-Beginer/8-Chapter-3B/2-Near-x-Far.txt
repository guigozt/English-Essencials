                Near (Perto)        |        Far (Longe)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Singular        This                |        That
                "Is this...?"       |        "What is that...?"

Plural          These               |        Those
                "Are these...?"     |        "What are those...?"


Examples:

1.  This is my phone     (Esse é o meu celular) ->  Near
2.  These are my friends (Estes são meus amigos) -> Near

3.  That is your car     (Aquele é o seu carro) ->  Far
4.  Those are mountains  (Aquelas são montanhas) -> Far
