What do you like doing?

    1.  I like reading in my free time
    2.  I don't like going to the gym
    3.  I love going out with my friends
    4.  I hate getting up early

Verb + ing

    Infinite    |   Verb + ing              |   Spelling
    -----------------------------------------------------------------------
    read        | I like reading            |   + ing
    fly         | You like flying           |   + ing
    ------------------------------------------------------------------------
    cycle       | He loves cycling          |   -e +ing
    drive       | They hate driving         |   -e +ing
    ------------------------------------------------------------------------
    swim        | We like swimming          |   one vowel + one consonant = 
    shop        | She doesn't like shopping |   double consonant + ing
