# Exercise 1: Verb + -ing Forms

Complete as lacunas utilizando a forma correta em **-ing** do verbo ou expressão entre parênteses:

---

### Questões

1. We love \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ in the forest. *(camp)*
2. She hates \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ in cold water. *(swim)*
3. They like \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ on weekends. *(shop)*
4. I don't like \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ on Friday nights. *(go to the gym)*
5. He doesn't like \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ in the morning. *(run)*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas Ortográficas</b></summary>

<br>

1. We love **camping** in the forest.  
   * *Regra:* Caso geral $\rightarrow$ adiciona-se apenas **-ing** (*camp* $\rightarrow$ *camping*).

2. She hates **swimming** in cold water.  
   * *Regra:* CVC (consoante-vogal-consoante: *w-i-m*) $\rightarrow$ dobra a consoante final (*swim* $\rightarrow$ *swimming*).

3. They like **shopping** on weekends.  
   * *Regra:* CVC (*h-o-p*) $\rightarrow$ dobra a consoante final (*shop* $\rightarrow$ *shopping*).

4. I don't like **going to the gym** on Friday nights.  
   * *Regra:* O verbo principal recebe o sufixo (*go* $\rightarrow$ *going*).

5. He doesn't like **running** in the morning.  
   * *Regra:* CVC (*r-u-n*) $\rightarrow$ dobra a consoante final (*run* $\rightarrow$ *running*).
