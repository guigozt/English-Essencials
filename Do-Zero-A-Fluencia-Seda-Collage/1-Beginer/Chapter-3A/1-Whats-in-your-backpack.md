# What's in Your Backpack?

Diálogos práticos sobre pertences pessoais em bolsas e mochilas.

---

### Diálogo 1: Bolsa de Mão (*Purse*)

- **Police officer:** *What's in your purse?*
- **Carol:** *It's a mobile phone.*
- **Police officer:** *Oh, no problem.*

---

### Diálogo 2: Mochila (*Backpack*)

- **Police officer:** *What's in your backpack?*
- **Tony:** *I have an umbrella, keys, brushes, and a laptop in my backpack.*
- **Police officer:** *You're ok. Bye!*

---

### Vocabulário Chave

- **Purse:** Bolsa (feminina / de mão)
- **Backpack:** Mochila
