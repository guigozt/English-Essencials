# Exercise 2: Put the Words in the Plural

Passe os seguintes substantivos para o plural e monte frases completas (*They're...*):

---

### Questões

1. **Watch**
2. **Country**
3. **Umbrella**
4. **Brush**

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta</b></summary>

<br>

1. **Watch** $\rightarrow$ **Watches** (*They're watches.*)
2. **Country** $\rightarrow$ **Countries** (*They're countries.*)
3. **Umbrella** $\rightarrow$ **Umbrellas** (*They're umbrellas.*)
4. **Brush** $\rightarrow$ **Brushes** (*They're brushes.*)

</details>
