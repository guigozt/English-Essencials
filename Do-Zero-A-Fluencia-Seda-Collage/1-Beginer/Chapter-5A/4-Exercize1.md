# Exercise 1: Adjectives with "It's" & "They're"

Forme frases completas usando **"It's a/an..."** (para o singular) ou **"They're..."** (para o plural) de acordo com os termos entre parênteses:

---

### Questões

1. (cars, old) $\rightarrow$
2. (cities, big) $\rightarrow$
3. (t-shirts, black) $\rightarrow$
4. (glasses, expensive) $\rightarrow$
5. (mobile phone, blue) $\rightarrow$

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas</b></summary>

<br>

1. **They're old cars.** *(São carros antigos)*
2. **They're big cities.** *(São cidades grandes)*
3. **They're black T-shirts.** *(São camisetas pretas)*
4. **They're expensive glasses.** *(São óculos caros — lembre-se: glasses é sempre plural)*
5. **It's a blue mobile phone.** *(É um celular azul)*

</details>
