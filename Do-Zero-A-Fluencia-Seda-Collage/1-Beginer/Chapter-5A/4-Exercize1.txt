Make phrases using "It's, a/an or They're"

    1.  (cars, old)
    2.  (cities, big)
    3.  (t-shirts, black)
    4.  (glasses, expensive)
    5.  (mobile, phone, blue)

Answer:

    1.  They're old cars
    2.  They're big cities
    3.  They're black t-shirts 
    4.  They're expensive glasses 
    5.  It's a blue mobile phone
