# Exercise 1: Present Continuous Sentences

Complete as lacunas com a forma correta do **Present Continuous** utilizando os verbos entre parênteses:

---

### Questões

1. He \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ a shower right now. *(not / have)*
2. You \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ the wrong exercise! *(do)*
3. He \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ tennis with his brother. *(play)*
4. They \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ in the hotel pool. *(swim)*
5. She \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ at home today. *(not / work)*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

1. He **isn't having** a shower right now.  
   * *Justificativa:* Terceira pessoa singular (*He*) com negativa $\rightarrow$ *isn't + having* (remove o *-e* do verbo *have*).

2. You **are doing** the wrong exercise!  
   * *Justificativa:* Sujeito *You* $\rightarrow$ *are + doing*.

3. He **is playing** tennis with his brother. *(ou contraído: **He's playing**)*  
   * *Justificativa:* Sujeito *He* $\rightarrow$ *is + playing*.

4. They **are swimming** in the hotel pool. *(ou contraído: **They're swimming**)*  
   * *Justificativa:* Sujeito *They* $\rightarrow$ *are + swimming* (CVC: dobra o *m*).

5. She **isn't working** at home today. *(ou **is not working**)*  
   * *Justificativa:* Sujeito *She* na negativa $\rightarrow$ *isn't + working*.
