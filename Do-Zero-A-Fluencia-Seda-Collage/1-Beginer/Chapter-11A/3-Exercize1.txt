Write sentences in the Present Continuous for each number

    1.  He  _   (have, not) a shower
    2.  You _   (do) the wrong exercize
    3.  He  _   (play) tennis
    4.  They _  (swim) in the pool
    5.  She _   (work, not) at home today

Answers:

    1.  He isn't having a shower
    2.  You are doing the wrong exercize
    3.  He's playing tennis
    4.  They're swimming in the pool
    5.  She isn't working at home today
