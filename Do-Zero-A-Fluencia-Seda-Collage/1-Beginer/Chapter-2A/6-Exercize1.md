# Exercise 1: Pronouns in the Plural

Complete as frases com o pronome adequado com a contração (**You're**, **They're**, **We're**):

---

### Questões

1. *(You and Sarah)* \_\_\_'re from the U.S.A.
2. *(Leo and Daniel)* \_\_\_'re from Brazil.
3. *(Lee and I)* \_\_\_'re Chinese.
4. *(Jim and I)* \_\_\_'re American.

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta</b></summary>

<br>

1. *(You and Sarah)* **You're** from the U.S.A.
2. *(Leo and Daniel)* **They're** from Brazil.
3. *(Lee and I)* **We're** Chinese.
4. *(Jim and I)* **We're** American.

</details>
