# We Are English (Part 2)

Expressões de viagem: férias (*on holiday*) vs trabalho/negócios (*on business*).

---

### Diálogo

- **Jane:** *Where are you from in England?*
- **Rachel:** *We're from London. Are you on holiday?*
- **Joe:** *London's a great city!*
- **Jane:** *No, we aren't. We are on business.*

---

### Vocabulário Útil

- **On holiday:** De férias
- **On business:** A negócios / A trabalho
