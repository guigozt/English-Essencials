# Verb To Be: We, You & They (Plural)

Formas afirmativas e negativas do verbo *to be* no plural.

---

### 1. Forma Afirmativa (+)

| Sujeito | Forma Completa (*Full Form*) | Forma Contraída (*Contraction*) | Exemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **We** | **We are** | **We're** | *We're German.* |
| **You** | **You are** | **You're** | *You're Spanish.* |
| **They** | **They are** | **They're** | *They're Japanese.* |

---

### 2. Forma Negativa (-)

| Sujeito | Forma Completa (*Full Form*) | Formas Contraídas (*Contractions*) | Exemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **We** | **We are not** | **We aren't** / **We're not** | *We aren't German.* |
| **You** | **You are not** | **You aren't** / **You're not** | *You aren't Spanish.* |
| **They** | **They are not** | **They aren't** / **They're not** | *They're not Japanese.* |
