# Exercise 1: Irregular Verbs in the Past Simple

Reescreva as frases no **Past Simple** incorporando a expressão de tempo indicada entre parênteses:

---

### Questões

a) I have a sandwich for breakfast. *(today)*  
b) They don't have dinner at home. *(last night)*  
c) What time do you get up? *(this morning)*  
d) Do you do sport or exercise? *(last week)*  
e) We don't do the housework. *(yesterday)*  

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

a) **Today, I had a sandwich for breakfast.** *(ou: I had a sandwich for breakfast today.)*  
* *Justificativa:* Passado irregular afirmativo de *have* $\rightarrow$ **had**.

b) **They didn't have dinner at home last night.**  
* *Justificativa:* Negativa no passado usa **didn't** + verbo base (*have*). Não se usa preposição antes de *last night* (sem "in").

c) **What time did you get up this morning?**  
* *Justificativa:* Pergunta com pronome interrogativo $\rightarrow$ *What time* + auxiliar **did** + sujeito + verbo base (*get up*).

d) **Did you do sport or exercise last week?**  
* *Justificativa:* Pergunta no passado $\rightarrow$ auxiliar **did** + sujeito + verbo base (*do*). Não usa "in" antes de *last week*.

e) **We didn't do the housework yesterday.**  
* *Justificativa:* Negativa $\rightarrow$ **didn't** + verbo base (*do*).

</details>
