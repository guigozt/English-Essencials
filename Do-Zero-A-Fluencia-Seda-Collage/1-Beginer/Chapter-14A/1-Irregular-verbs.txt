Irregular Verbs

    Example:

    -   How was your day?
    -   I had a great day!

    Dialogue:

        In the morning: 
        -   I got up at 9 o'clock/ I had breakfast / I had a shower / I went to school

        In the afternoon:
        -   I had lunch and a coffee/ I went home/ I did my homework

        In the evening:
        -   I went shopping/ I did yoga/ I had dinner/ I went to bed
