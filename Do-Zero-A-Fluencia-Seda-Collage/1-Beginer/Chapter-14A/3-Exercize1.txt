1- Write the sentences in the past simple:

    a) I have a sandwich for breakfast. (today)
    b) They don't have dinner at home. ( last night)
    c) What time do you get up?  (this morning)
    d) Do you do sport or exercise? (last week)
    e) We don't do the housework. (yesterday)

Answers:

    1.  Today, I had a sandwich for breakfast
    2.  They didn't have dinner at home in last night
    3.  What time did you get up this morning?
    4.  Did you do sport or exercise in last week?
    5.  We didn't do the housework, yesterday.
