# Past Simple: Affirmative, Negative & Questions

Como construir frases afirmativas, negativas e perguntas com verbos irregulares no passado.

---

### 1. Estrutura Gramatical Completa

| Sujeito | Afirmativa (+) | Negativa (-) | Perguntas & Respostas (?) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I** | *I **did** my homework.* | *I **didn't do** my homework.* | **What did you do last Saturday?**<br>$\rightarrow$ *I went out with my friends.* |
| **You** | *You **went** out.* | *You **didn't go** out.* | |
| **He / She / It** | *She **got** up early.* | *She **didn't get** up early.* | **Did you get up early?**<br>• *Yes, I did.*<br>• *No, I didn't.* |
| **We** | *We **had** lunch.* | *We **didn't have** lunch.* | |
| **They** | *They **did** sport.* | *They **didn't do** sport.* | |

---

### 2. Dica de Ouro com Auxiliar DID

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Em frases **negativas** e **interrogativas**, o verbo principal volta para a **forma base (infinitivo)**:
> * ❌ *I didn't went home.* $\rightarrow$ ✅ **I didn't go home.**
> * ❌ *Did you had lunch?* $\rightarrow$ ✅ **Did you have lunch?**
> * ❌ *She didn't got up.* $\rightarrow$ ✅ **She didn't get up.**
