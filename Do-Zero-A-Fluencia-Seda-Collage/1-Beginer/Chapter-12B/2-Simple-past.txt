Simple Past + Verb to Be

                +                   |               -
    --------------------------------------------------------------------
    I was a waiter                  |   I wasn't a teacher
    You were in class today         |   You weren't in class yesterday 
    She/He/It was tired             |   She/He/It wasn't tired
    We were in London last week     |   We weren't in Brazil last month
    You were late this morning      |   You weren't late last class
    They were famous in the 80's    |   They weren't famous in the past

Simple Past (Interrogatives)

    Were they late?
    +   Yes, They were
    -   No, They weren't

    Was she a singer?
    +   Yes, She was
    -   No, she wasn't
