Complete the sentences using was, were, wasn't or weren't and on, in, at

    1.  I _____ ____ a bus _____ 7 o'clock. (-)
    2.  She _____ a singer _____ the 90's.  (+)
    3.  We _____ _____ home yesterday. (-)
    4.  You _____ _____ the museum. (+)
    5.  My dog _____ _____ the kitchen. (-)

Answers

    1.  I wasn't on a bus at 7 o'clock
    2.  She was a singer in the 90's
    3.  We weren't at home yesterday
    4.  You were in the museum
    5.  My dog wasn't in the kitchen
