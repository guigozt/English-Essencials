### Questão

- **A:** \_\_\_\_\_ *"anjo"* in \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_?
- **B:** It's angel
<br>

- **A:** \_\_\_\_\_ do you \_\_\_\_\_\_ it?

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta</b></summary>

<br>
