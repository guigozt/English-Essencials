# Exercise 2: Write the Numbers

Escreva os seguintes números por extenso em inglês:

---

### Questões

1. **52**
2. **42**
3. **89**
4. **72**
5. **69**
6. **28**
7. **34**
8. **92**

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta</b></summary>

<br>

1. **52** $\rightarrow$ **fifty-two**
2. **42** $\rightarrow$ **forty-two**
3. **89** $\rightarrow$ **eighty-nine**
4. **72** $\rightarrow$ **seventy-two**
5. **69** $\rightarrow$ **sixty-nine**
6. **28** $\rightarrow$ **twenty-eight**
7. **34** $\rightarrow$ **thirty-four**
8. **92** $\rightarrow$ **ninety-two**

</details>
