# Exercise 1: Wh- and How Words

Complete as lacunas com a palavra interrogativa correta (*Who, When, How, Where, What*):

---

### Questões

1. \_\_\_'s he?
2. \_\_\_'s the show?
3. \_\_\_ old are you?
4. \_\_\_ are you?
5. \_\_\_'s he from?
6. \_\_\_'s your name?

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta</b></summary>

<br>

1. **Who's** he?
2. **When's** the show?
3. **How** old are you?
4. **How** are you?
5. **Where's** he from?
6. **What's** your name?

</details>
