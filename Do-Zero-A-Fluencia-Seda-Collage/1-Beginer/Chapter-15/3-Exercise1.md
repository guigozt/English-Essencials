# Exercise 1: Present Continuous for Future Arrangements

Complete as lacunas utilizando a forma do **Present Continuous com sentido de futuro** para os verbos entre parênteses:

---

### Questões

1. Mark \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ on Friday at 10 p.m. *(arrive)*
2. What time \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ you \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to the doctor's tomorrow? *(go)*
3. My father \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ this afternoon because he is very busy. *(not / come)*
4. \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ we \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ your friends tonight? *(meet)*
5. What time \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ you \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ today? *(leave)*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

1. Mark **is arriving** on Friday at 10 p.m. *(ou contraído: **Mark's arriving**)*  
   * *Justificativa:* Sujeito singular na 3ª pessoa (*Mark*) com horário marcado $\rightarrow$ *is arriving*.

2. What time **are** you **going** to the doctor's tomorrow?  
   * *Justificativa:* Pergunta com *you* para plano futuro de amanhã $\rightarrow$ *are you going*.

3. My father **isn't coming** this afternoon. He's busy.  
   * *Justificativa:* Negativa para plano cancelado $\rightarrow$ *isn't coming* (remove o *-e* de *come*).

4. **Are** we **meeting** your friends tonight?  
   * *Justificativa:* Pergunta sobre plano combinado para hoje à noite $\rightarrow$ *Are we meeting*.

5. What time **are** you **leaving** today?  
   * *Justificativa:* Pergunta sobre horário agendado de partida $\rightarrow$ *What time are you leaving*.

</details>
