# Exercise 2: Writing Ordinal Numbers

Escreva os seguintes números ordinais por extenso em inglês:

---

### Questões

1. **1st** $\rightarrow$
2. **23rd** $\rightarrow$
3. **28th** $\rightarrow$
4. **13th** $\rightarrow$
5. **31st** $\rightarrow$
6. **11th** $\rightarrow$
7. **30th** $\rightarrow$
8. **15th** $\rightarrow$
9. **20th** $\rightarrow$

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas</b></summary>

<br>

1. 1st $\rightarrow$ **First**
2. 23rd $\rightarrow$ **Twenty-third**
3. 28th $\rightarrow$ **Twenty-eighth**
4. 13th $\rightarrow$ **Thirteenth**
5. 31st $\rightarrow$ **Thirty-first**
6. 11th $\rightarrow$ **Eleventh**
7. 30th $\rightarrow$ **Thirtieth**
8. 15th $\rightarrow$ **Fifteenth**
9. 20th $\rightarrow$ **Twentieth**

</details>
