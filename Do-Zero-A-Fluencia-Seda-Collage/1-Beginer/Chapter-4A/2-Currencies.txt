Currencies

    -   euro / euros -> Moeda Euro
    -   pounds ->       Moeda Libra
    -   dollars ->      Moeda Dolar

    Example 1:

        How much is it? ->  It's five dollars and fifty cents
        
        -   Comum: I'ts five fifty ($5.50)

    Example 2:
        How much is it? ->  It's twelve pounds and seventy pence (12,70)

        -   Comum: It's twelve seventy (£12.70)
