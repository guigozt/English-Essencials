# Exercise 1: Writing Prices

Escreva os preços por extenso em inglês com a moeda indicada entre parênteses:

---

### Questões

1. It's **5,20** *(dollars)*
2. It's **20,14** *(euros)*
3. They're **31,80** *(pounds)*
4. They're **95,95** *(dollars)*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas</b></summary>

<br>

1. It's **five dollars and twenty cents** *(ou: five twenty)*
2. It's **twenty euros and fourteen cents** *(ou: twenty fourteen)*
3. They're **thirty-one pounds and eighty pence** *(ou: thirty-one eighty)*
4. They're **ninety-five dollars and ninety-five cents** *(ou: ninety-five ninety-five)*
