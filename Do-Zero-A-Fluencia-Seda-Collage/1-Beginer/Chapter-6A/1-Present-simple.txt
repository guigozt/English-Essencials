📚 Present Simple (I, You, We, They)

    When do we use it?
    -   We use the Present Simple to talk about:

    ✅ Habits
    -   I drink coffee every morning.

    ✅ Routines
    -   We go to school by bus.

    ✅ Facts
    -   They live in Brazil.

    ✅ Likes and preferences
    -   You like pop music.

📝 The Rule
    
    For these subjects:
    -   I
    -   You
    -   We
    -   They

    The verb stays in its base form.
    Unlike He, She, It, we do not add "s".

    | Subject | Verb   |
    | ------- | ------ |
    | I       | work   |
    | You     | work   |
    | We      | work   |
    | They    | work   |

    Example:

    | Subject |         +           |            -               |          ?              |
    | ------- | ------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
    | I       | live in Brazil.     | don't live in Brazil       | Do i live in Brazil?    |
    | You     | live in Brazil      | don't live in Brazil       | Do you live in Brazil?  |
    | We      | live in Brazil      | don't live in Brazil       | Do we live in Brazil?   |
    | They    | live in Brazil      | don't live in Brazil       | Do they live in Brazil? |

📌 Short Answers

    | Question               | Positive      | Negative        |
    | ---------------------- | ------------- | --------------- |
    | Do you live in Brazil? | Yes, you do.  | No, you don't.  |
    | Do they watch TV?      | Yes, they do. | No, they don't. |
    | Do we need a new car?  | Yes, we do.   | No, we don't.   |
    | Do I read books?       | Yes, I do.    | No, I don't.    |

💡 The Auxiliary Verb "Do"

    In the Present Simple, do helps us make questions and negative sentences.

    Questions
    -   Do you speak English?
    -   Do they work here?

    Negatives
    -   I don't speak English.
    -   We don't work here.

    ⚠️ Do does not have a meaning here. It only helps build the sentence.
