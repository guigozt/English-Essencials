🍽️ Meals of the Day

    What are the main meals of the day?

1. Breakfast
    -   Meaning: Café da manhã.
    -   The first meal of the day, usually eaten in the morning.

    Common question
    -   What do you have for breakfast?
    
    Example answers
    -   I have eggs and juice for breakfast.
    -   She has cereal and milk for breakfast.

2. Brunch
    📌 Brunch = breakfast + lunch
    -   Meaning: A combination of breakfast and lunch.
    -   It is usually eaten late in the morning or early in the afternoon.

    Example
    -   We have brunch on Sundays.
    -   They have pancakes and coffee for brunch.

    Common brunch foods

    -   pancakes
    -   eggs
    -   bacon
    -   sandwiches
    -   fruit
    -   coffee

3. Lunch
    -   Meaning: Almoço.
    -   Usually eaten around midday.

    Common question
    -   What do you have for lunch?

    Example answers
    -   They have rice, steak, beans, and salad for lunch.
    -   I have pasta for lunch.
    -   We usually have chicken and vegetables for lunch.

4. Dinner
    -   Meaning: Jantar.
    -   Usually the main evening meal.

    Common question
    -   What do you have for dinner?

    Example answers
    -   I have soup for dinner.
    -   We have pizza for dinner.
    -   They have fish and rice for dinner.

    Common dinner foods
    -   soup
    -   pizza
    -   fish
    -   chicken
    -   rice
    -   vegetables

5. Supper
    -   Meaning: Ceia.
    
    -   In modern English, supper is less common than dinner.
    -   Depending on the country and region, it can mean:
        -   a light meal before bed;
        -   the evening meal (especially in some parts of the UK and rural areas).

    Examples
    -   I have a light supper before bed.
    -   My grandparents call dinner "supper."
    
    💡 For everyday conversations, dinner is much more common.

6. Snack
    -   Meaning: Lanche rápido.
    -   A small meal eaten between main meals.

    Examples
    -   I have a snack in the afternoon.
    -   She eats fruit as a snack.
