🍴 Asking About Meals

    A common pattern is:
    -   What do you have for + meal?

    Examples
    -   What do you have for breakfast?
    -   What do you have for lunch?
    -   What do you have for dinner?

    Possible answers
    -   I have + food + for + meal.

    -   I have eggs for breakfast.
    -   I have rice and beans for lunch.
    -   I have soup for dinner.
