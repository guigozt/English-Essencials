# Exercise 1: Past Simple with Regular and Irregular Verbs

Complete as frases abaixo colocando os verbos entre parênteses na forma correta do **Past Simple** (afirmativa, negativa ou interrogativa):

---

### Questões

1. The old man \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ my husband to wait outside. *(tell)*
2. He \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ for his lunch. *(not / pay)*
3. She \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ today. *(not / arrive)*
4. Where \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ you \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ his sister? *(see)*
5. I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, but I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to see his face. *(travel / not want)*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

1. The old man **told** my husband to wait outside.  
   * *Justificativa:* Verbo irregular afirmativo de *tell* $\rightarrow$ **told**.

2. He **didn't pay** for his lunch.  
   * *Justificativa:* Negativa no passado $\rightarrow$ **didn't** + verbo base (*pay*).

3. She **didn't arrive** today.  
   * *Justificativa:* Negativa no passado $\rightarrow$ **didn't** + verbo base regular (*arrive*).

4. Where **did** you **see** his sister?  
   * *Justificativa:* Pergunta no passado $\rightarrow$ *Where* + auxiliar **did** + sujeito (*you*) + verbo base (*see*).

5. I **travelled**, but I **didn't want** to see his face.  
   * *Justificativa:* Verbo regular afirmativo dobra o "l" no inglês britânico (*travelled* / no AmE: *traveled*); a segunda parte é negativa com **didn't want**.

</details>
