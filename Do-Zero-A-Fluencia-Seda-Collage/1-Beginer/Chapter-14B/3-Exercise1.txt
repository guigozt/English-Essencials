1- Complete the sentences with the verbs in brackets (in the past simple form):

    1-  The old man _____ (tell) my husband to wait.
    2-  He _____ (not, pay) for his lunch.
    3- She _____ (not, arrive) today.
    4- Where _____ you _____ (see) his sister?
    5- I _____ (travel), but I _____ (not, want) to see his face.

Answers:

    1.  The old man told my husband to wait
    2.  He didn't pay for his lunch
    3.  She didn't arrive today
    4.  Where did you see his sister?
    5.  I travelled, but I didn't want to see his face.
