Write the time (use to, past half or o'clock)

    1.  06:00 am
    2.  07:30 am
    3.  02:15 pm
    4.  06:45 pm
    5.  01:20 pm

Answers:

    1.  06:00 am    ->  It's six o'clock
    2.  07:30 am    ->  Its half past seven
    3.  02:15 pm    ->  It's a quarter past two
    4.  06:45 pm    ->  It's a quarter to seven
    5.  01:20 pm    ->  It's twenty past one
