🕒 What's the time?

    📚 Common Questions

    | Question         | Meaning                                            |
    | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
    | What time is it? | Que horas são?                                     |
    | What's the time? | Que horas são? *(More common in British English.)* |


💬 Dialogue

**John:** *What's the time, Paul?*
**Paul:** *It's half past eight. Do you need to go?*
**John:** *Yes, I have a meeting in Dublin tomorrow morning at a quarter to eight... 
            or eight o'clock... I can't remember.*

⏰ O'Clock
    Use **o'clock** when the minutes are **00**.

    | Time  | Expression           |
    | ----- | -------------------- |
    | 1:00  | It's one o'clock.    |
    | 2:00  | It's two o'clock.    |
    | 5:00  | It's five o'clock.   |
    | 8:00  | It's eight o'clock.  |
    | 12:00 | It's twelve o'clock. |

🕒 Past
    Use **past** when the minutes are **from 01 to 30**.

    | Time | Expression                   |
    | ---- | ---------------------------- |
    | 5:10 | It's ten past five.          |
    | 3:20 | It's twenty past three.      |
    | 7:25 | It's twenty-five past seven. |

    💡 **Past = after the hour.**

🕒 To
    Use **to** when the minutes are **from 31 to 59**.

    | Time | Expression             |
    | ---- | ---------------------- |
    | 5:40 | It's twenty to six.    |
    | 5:45 | It's a quarter to six. |
    | 2:50 | It's ten to three.     |
    | 2:55 | It's five to three.    |

    💡 **To = before the next hour.**

⌚ Quarter
    A **quarter** means **15 minutes**.

    | Time  | Expression                 |
    | ----- | -------------------------- |
    | 5:15  | It's a quarter past five.  |
    | 7:15  | It's a quarter past seven. |
    | 5:45  | It's a quarter to six.     |
    | 11:45 | It's a quarter to twelve.  |

    📌 **Quarter = 15 minutes**

⌚ Half
    **Half** means **30 minutes**.

    | Time  | Expression            |
    | ----- | --------------------- |
    | 7:30  | It's half past seven. |
    | 10:30 | It's half past ten.   |
    | 1:30  | It's half past one.   |

    📌 **Half = 30 minutes**
