# Verb To Be: He, She & It (Present Simple)

Estruturas do verbo *to be* (ser/estar) para a terceira pessoa do singular: **He** (ele), **She** (ela) e **It** (ele/ela para coisas, objetos e animais).

---

### 1. Forma Afirmativa (+)

| Sujeito | Forma Completa (*Full Form*) | Forma Contraída (*Contraction*) | Exemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **He** | **He is** | **He's** | *He's from Brazil.* |
| **She** | **She is** | **She's** | *She's from Brazil.* |
| **It** | **It is** | **It's** | *It's from Brazil.* |

---

### 2. Forma Negativa (-)

| Sujeito | Forma Completa (*Full Form*) | Formas Contraídas (*Contractions*) | Exemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **He** | **He is not** | **He's not** / **He isn't** | *He isn't from France.* |
| **She** | **She is not** | **She's not** / **She isn't** | *She isn't from France.* |
| **It** | **It is not** | **It's not** / **It isn't** | *It isn't from France.* |
