# Exercise 2: Negative and Interrogative Forms

Transforme as frases afirmativas abaixo para a **forma negativa** e para a **forma interrogativa**:

---

### Frases Base

1. *He's from Spain.*
2. *She's from Japan.*
3. *She's from Turkey.*
4. *It's from the U.S.A.*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta: Forma Negativa (-)</b></summary>

<br>

1. **He's not from Spain.** *(ou: He isn't from Spain.)*
2. **She's not from Japan.** *(ou: She isn't from Japan.)*
3. **She isn't from Turkey.** *(ou: She's not from Turkey.)*
4. **It's not from the U.S.A.** *(ou: It isn't from the U.S.A.)*

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta: Forma Interrogativa (?)</b></summary>

<br>

1. **Is he from Spain?**
2. **Is she from Japan?**
3. **Is she from Turkey?**
4. **Is it from the U.S.A.?**

</details>
