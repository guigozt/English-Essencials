# Where Is He From? (Where Are You From?)

Diálogos práticos para perguntar sobre origem e nacionalidade de pessoas e lugares.

---

### Diálogo 1: Perguntando a Origem de Alguém e da Cidade

- **Bruce:** *Where are you from?*
- **Jade:** *I'm from Madrid.*
- **Bruce:** *Where's it?*
- **Jade:** *It's in Spain.*

---

### Diálogo 2: Perguntando sobre Ele (*He*)

- **Bruce:** *Where's he from?*
- **Jade:** *I think he's from China.*
- **Tim:** *Yes! He's from China.*
- **Bruce:** *He's fantastic!*

---

### Diálogo 3: Perguntando sobre Ela (*She*)

- **Bruce:** *Where's she from?*
- **Jade:** *I think she's from the U.S.A.*
- **Tim:** *Yes! She's from the U.S.A.*
- **Bruce:** *She's fantastic!*
