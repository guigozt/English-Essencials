# Exercise 1: He, She or It

Complete as sentenças utilizando o pronome correto (**he**, **she** ou **it**):

---

### Questões

1. *(Robert)* \_\_\_'s from Spain.
2. *(Jade)* \_\_\_'s from Japan.
3. *(Jenny)* \_\_\_'s from Turkey.
4. *(Snowball the cat)* \_\_\_'s from the U.S.A.

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta</b></summary>

<br>

1. *(Robert)* **He's** from Spain.
2. *(Jade)* **She's** from Japan.
3. *(Jenny)* **She's** from Turkey.
4. *(Snowball the cat)* **It's** from the U.S.A.

</details>
