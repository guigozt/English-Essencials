# Exercise 3: Complete the Dialogue

Complete o diálogo e as palavras incompletas utilizando contrações quando apropriado:

---

### Questão

- **Rapha:** "\_\_\_'s \_\_\_ from?"
- **John:** "I \_\_\_ Jenny's from \_re\_d."
- **Anna:** "No, she \_\_\_. She's from the \_\_\_ and she \_\_\_ fa\_ta\_!"

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta</b></summary>

<br>

- **Rapha:** *Where's she from?*
- **John:** *I think Jenny's from Ireland.*
- **Anna:** *No, she isn't. She's from the U.K. and she's fantastic!*
