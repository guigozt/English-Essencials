Dialogue:

    Tourist: "Excuse me! Where's the bank?"
    Police officer: "Oh, The bank on the corner of King Street and Queen Street"
    Tourist: "Great! And... Where's the restaurant?"
    Police officer: "It's next to the train station"

Asking for directions and places

    | Expression           | Meaning                     | Example                                                                 |
    | -------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
    | **between**          | entre                       | The bar is **between** the bank and the gym.                            |
    | **on the left**      | à esquerda                  | The gym is **on the left side of Jack Street.**                         |
    | **on the right**     | à direita                   | The hotel is **on the right side of Jack Street.**                      |
    | **opposite**         | em frente a / do outro lado | The restaurant is **opposite** the bar.                                 |
    | **next to**          | ao lado de                  | The train station is **next to** the hospital.                          |
    | **on the corner of** | na esquina de               | The train station is **on the corner of Queen Street and King Street.** |
