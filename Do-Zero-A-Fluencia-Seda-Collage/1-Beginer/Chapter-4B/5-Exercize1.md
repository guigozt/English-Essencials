# Exercise 1: Possessive Adjectives

Complete os espaços em branco com o adjetivo possessivo correto (**my**, **your**, **his**, **her**, **our**, **their**):

---

### Questões

1. I'm Irish. \_\_\_ name's Sean.
2. He's my friend. \_\_\_ name's Jack.
3. They're Mexican. \_\_\_ names are Andre and Ben.
4. We are in the room. This is \_\_\_ key.
5. She's my sister. \_\_\_ name's Kelly.

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas</b></summary>

<br>

1. I'm Irish. **My** name's Sean.
2. He's my friend. **His** name's Jack.
3. They're Mexican. **Their** names are Andre and Ben.
4. We are in the room. This is **our** key.
5. She's my sister. **Her** name's Kelly.

</details>
