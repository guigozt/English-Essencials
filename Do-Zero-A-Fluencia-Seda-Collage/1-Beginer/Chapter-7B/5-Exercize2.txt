Complete the sentences

    1.  I like coffee _ I don't like milk  
    2.  I have steak _ rice for lunch
    3.  I stay at work _ 10 o'clock
    4.  I have breakfast _ 9 o'clock
    5.  I get up at 6 _ I start school at 7 
    6.  I have a shower _ I go to sleep

Answers

    1.  I like coffee but I don't like milk  
    2.  I have steak and rice for lunch
    3.  I stay at work until 10 o'clock
    4.  I have breakfast about 9 o'clock
    5.  I get up at 6 because I start school at 7 
    6.  I have a shower before I go to sleep
