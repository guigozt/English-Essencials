📚 Adverbs of Frequency
    Adverbs of Frequency tell us how often an action happens.
    They are commonly used with the Present Simple to describe habits, routines, and repeated actions.

📊 Common Adverbs of Frequency

    | Adverb    | Meaning                  | Approximate Frequency |
    | --------- | ------------------------ | --------------------: |
    | Always    | Sempre                   |                  100% |
    | Usually   | Geralmente / Normalmente |                80–90% |
    | Sometimes | Às vezes                 |                40–50% |
    | Never*    | Nunca                    |                    0% |

📝 Sentence Structure

    For most verbs:
    
    Subject + Adverb + Verb
       I      always    get up early.
      She     usually   studies at night.
      They   sometimes  eat out.

    📌 The adverb usually comes before the main verb.

    ❗ Exception: Verb "To Be"
    
    When using to be, the adverb comes after the verb.

    | Correct                | Incorrect                |
    | ---------------------- | ------------------------ |
    | She is always happy.   | She always is happy. ❌   |
    | They are usually late. | They usually are late. ❌ |

1. Always
    -   Meaning: Sempre
    -   Used for actions that happen all the time or almost all the time.

    Question:
    -   What time do you get up?

    Answer:
    -   I always get up at 7 o'clock because I start work at 8.

    More Examples
    
    Question:
    -   What time do you go to school?   

    Answer:
    -   I always go to school at 7 o'clock (p.m) But I hate it. I never late.

2. Usually
    -   Meaning: Geralmente / Normalmente
    -   Used for actions that happen most of the time.

    Question:
    -   Do you have a shower in the morning?

    Answer:
    -   I usually have a shower in the morning.

    More Examples
    -   I usually go to bed at 11 p.m.
    -   They usually have lunch at home.
    -   He usually watches TV after dinner.

3. Sometimes
    -   Meaning: Às vezes
    -   Used for actions that happen occasionally.

    Question:
    -   Where do you have breakfast?

    Answer:
    -   I sometimes have breakfast at home or at work.

    More Examples
    -   I sometimes read before bed.
    -   She sometimes goes to the cinema.
    -   We sometimes eat pizza on Fridays.
