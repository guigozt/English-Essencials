# Exercise 2: Conjunctions and Prepositions

Complete as frases abaixo escolhendo a conjunção ou preposição de tempo mais adequada (**and**, **but**, **because**, **until**, **about**, **before**):

---

### Questões

1. I like coffee, \_\_\_ I don't like milk.
2. I have steak \_\_\_ rice for lunch.
3. I stay at work \_\_\_ 10 o'clock.
4. I have breakfast \_\_\_ 9 o'clock.
5. I get up at 6 \_\_\_ I start school at 7.
6. I have a shower \_\_\_ I go to sleep.

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas</b></summary>

<br>

1. I like coffee, **but** I don't like milk. *(contraste)*
2. I have steak **and** rice for lunch. *(adição)*
3. I stay at work **until** 10 o'clock. *(limite de tempo: até as 10)*
4. I have breakfast **about** 9 o'clock. *(aproximação: por volta das 9)*
5. I get up at 6 **because** I start school at 7. *(motivo: porque começo)*
6. I have a shower **before** I go to sleep. *(ordem temporal: antes de dormir)*
