# Exercise 1: Adverbs of Frequency

Complete as lacunas com o advérbio de frequência correto (**Always**, **Usually**, **Sometimes**, **Never**) com base nas porcentagens indicadas:

> **Lembrete de Porcentagens:**
> - **100%:** Always
> - **80–90%:** Usually
> - **40–50%:** Sometimes
> - **0%:** Never

---

### Questões

1. I \_\_\_ *(90%)* go to work at 9.
2. My wife \_\_\_ *(100%)* has juice for breakfast.
3. We \_\_\_ *(50%)* go to the cinema.
4. He \_\_\_ *(0%)* drinks coffee.

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas</b></summary>

<br>

1. I **usually** go to work at 9. *(Eu geralmente vou para o trabalho às 9)*
2. My wife **always** has juice for breakfast. *(Minha esposa sempre toma suco no café da manhã)*
3. We **sometimes** go to the cinema. *(Nós às vezes vamos ao cinema)*
4. He **never** drinks coffee. *(Ele nunca bebe café)*

</details>
