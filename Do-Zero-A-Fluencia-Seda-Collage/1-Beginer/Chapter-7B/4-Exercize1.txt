Use Always, usually, sometimes or never

    1.  I _ (90%) go to work at 9
    2.  My wife _ (100%) has juice for breakfast
    3.  We _ (50%) go to the cinema
    4.  He _ (0%) drinks coffee

Answers

    1.  I usually (90%) go to work at 9
    2.  My wife always (100%) has juice for breakfast
    3.  We sometimes (50%) go to the cinema
    4.  He never (0%) drinks coffee
