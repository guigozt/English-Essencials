📚 Conjunctions

Conjunctions are words that connect words, phrases, or sentences.

They help us express ideas more naturally.

📊 Common Conjunctions

    | Conjunction | Meaning | Function                  |
    | ----------- | ------- | ------------------------- |
    | **And**     | e       | Adds information.         |
    | **But**     | mas     | Shows contrast.           |
    | **Or**      | ou      | Gives options or choices. |
    | **Because** | porque  | Gives a reason.           |

1. And
    Meaning: e
    Use and to connect similar ideas or add information.

    Examples
    -   I like cereal and juice for breakfast.
    -   She speaks English and Spanish.
    -   We have lunch and then we go back to work.
    -   I get up early and go to work.

2. But
    Meaning: mas
    Use but to show contrast or opposite ideas.

    Examples
    -   I get up at 7, but I don't like it.
    -   He works a lot, but he loves his job.
    -   She is tired, but she keeps studying.
    -   I like coffee, but I don't like tea.

3. Or
    Meaning: ou
    Use or to offer choices or alternatives.

    Examples
    -   Do you want milk or tea?
    -   Would you like coffee or juice?
    -   Do you live in a house or a flat?
    -   Is your meeting today or tomorrow?

    📌 Or is very common in questions.

4. Because
    Meaning: porque
    Use because to explain a reason.

    Examples
    -   I get up at 6 because I start work at 7.
    -   She studies English because she wants a better job.
    -   We stay at home because it's raining.
    -   I go to bed early because I'm tired.

    📌 Because answers the question "Why?"
