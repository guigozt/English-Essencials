What do you have for breakfast?

    -   Uso do have ->  Contexto: Comer ou beber
    -   Healthy     ->  Saudável
    -   A lot of    ->  Significa "muito"

    "Hi, I'm Guilherme!
    I have breakfast at home. I have cereal, fruit, and yoghurt, and I drink espresso coffee with hot milk.
    I don't have eggs for breakfast.
    I like my breakfast. It's very healthy"
