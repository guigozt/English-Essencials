# Exercise 2: Present Simple (+ and -)

Escreva frases afirmativas $(+)$ ou negativas $(-)$ no Present Simple com as palavras indicadas:

---

### Questões

1. (We / coffee / breakfast) $(-)$ $\rightarrow$
2. (I / orange juice / breakfast) $(-)$ $\rightarrow$
3. (I / breakfast / home) $(+)$ $\rightarrow$
4. (You / tea / breakfast) $(+)$ $\rightarrow$

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas</b></summary>

<br>

1. **We don't have coffee for breakfast.** *(Nós não tomamos café no café da manhã)*
2. **I don't have orange juice for breakfast.** *(Eu não tomo suco de laranja no café da manhã)*
3. **I have breakfast at home.** *(Eu tomo café da manhã em casa)*
4. **You have tea for breakfast.** *(Você toma chá no café da manhã)*

</details>
