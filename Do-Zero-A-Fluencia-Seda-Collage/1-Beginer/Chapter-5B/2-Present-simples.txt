Present Simple

    ->  do ->   Auxiliar para negar (do not = don't)

            +                       |             -
    -------------------------------------------------------
    I have eggs for breakfast       |   I don't have...
    You have fruit for breakfast    |   You don't have...
    We have coffee for breakfast    |   We don't have...
    They have orange juice          |   They don't have
        for breakfast
