# Exercise 1: Prepositions (With, At, For)

Complete as frases usando as preposições corretas (**with**, **at** ou **for**):

> **Dicas de uso:**
> - **at:** para localização fixa (*at home*, *at work*)
> - **with:** para acompanhamento (*coffee with milk*)
> - **for:** para a ocasião / refeição (*for breakfast*)

---

### Questões

1. I have breakfast \_\_\_ home.
2. I have coffee \_\_\_ milk \_\_\_ breakfast.
3. I like tea \_\_\_ milk.
4. I don't like to have breakfast \_\_\_ work.

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas</b></summary>

<br>

1. I have breakfast **at** home. *(Tomo café da manhã em casa)*
2. I have coffee **with** milk **for** breakfast. *(Tomo café com leite no café da manhã)*
3. I like tea **with** milk. *(Gosto de chá com leite)*
4. I don't like to have breakfast **at** work. *(Não gosto de tomar café da manhã no trabalho)*
