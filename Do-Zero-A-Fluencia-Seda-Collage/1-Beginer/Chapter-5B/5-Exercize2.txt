Write + or - sentences

    1.  (We / coffee / breakfast) - 
    2.  (I / orange juice / breakfast) -
    3.  (I / breakfast / home) +
    4.  (You / tea / breakfast) + 

Answers:

    1.  We don't have coffee for breakfast 
    2.  I don't have orange juice for breakfast
    3.  I have breakfast at home
    4.  You have tea for breakfast
