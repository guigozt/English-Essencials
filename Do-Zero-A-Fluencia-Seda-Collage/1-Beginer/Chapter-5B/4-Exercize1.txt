Complete the phrases using with, at or for

    1.  I have breakfast _ home
    2.  I have coffee _ milk _ breakfast
    3.  I like tea _ milk
    4.  I don't like to have breakfast _ work

Answers:

    1.  I have breakfast at home
    2.  I have coffee with milk for breakfast
    3.  I like tea with milk
    4.  I don't like to have breakfast at work
