Can + How much

    -   Can -> Posso
            Usado para fazer pedidos de forma educada
    -   How much -> Quanto custa
            Perguntar o preço de algo

    Example 1:

        Barman: "Can I help you?"
        Jim: "Can I have an orange juice, please?"
        Barman: "There you go!"
        Jim: "Thanks! How much is it?"
        Barman: "It's five dollars"

    -   Here's your change ->   Aqui está seu troco  

    Example 2:

         Barman: "Here's your change"
         Jim: "Thanks! Have a nice day"
         Barman: You too!
