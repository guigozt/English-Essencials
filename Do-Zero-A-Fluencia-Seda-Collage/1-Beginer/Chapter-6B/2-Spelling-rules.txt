📝 Spelling Rules – Third Person Singular (Simple Present)

    When the subject is he, she, or it, the verb usually changes.

    1. Add -s
        Rule: Most verbs → + s

        | Base form         | Third person        |
        | ----------------- | ------------------- |
        | I live in Brazil. | He lives in Brazil. |
        | I work here.      | She works here.     |
        | I play soccer.    | It plays soccer.    |

    2. Add -es
        Rule: Verbs ending in ch, sh, ss, x, z, o → + es

        | Base form          | Third person          |
        | ------------------ | --------------------- |
        | I watch TV.        | She watches TV.       |
        | I wash the dishes. | He washes the dishes. |
        | I kiss my dog.     | She kisses her dog.   |
        | I fix the car.     | He fixes the car.     |
        | I go to school.    | She goes to school.   |

    3. Change -y to -ies
        Rule: Consonant + y → remove y and add ies.

        | Base form        | Third person        |
        | ---------------- | ------------------- |
        | I study History. | He studies History. |
        | I carry a bag.   | She carries a bag.  |
        | I try my best.   | He tries his best.  |


⚠️ Exception:
    If the verb ends in a vowel + y, just add -s

    | Base form      | Third person      |
    | -------------- | ----------------- |
    | I play soccer. | He plays soccer.  |
    | I enjoy music. | She enjoys music. |
    | I buy books.   | He buys books.    |
