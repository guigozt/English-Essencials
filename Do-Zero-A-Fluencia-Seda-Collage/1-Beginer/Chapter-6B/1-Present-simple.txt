📝 Present Simple (He, She, It)

    When do we use it?
    -   We use the Present Simple to talk about:

    ✅ Habits
    -   He drinks coffee every morning.

    ✅ Routines
    -   She goes to work by bus.

    ✅ Facts
    -   The Sun rises in the east.

    ✅ Jobs and occupations
    -   He works at Facebook.

📝 The Rule

    When the subject is:
    -   He
    -   She
    -   It

    The verb usually adds -s in affirmative sentences.

    | Subject | Verb   |
    | ------- | ------ |
    | He      | works  |
    | She     | speaks |
    | It      | eats   |

    Example:

    | Subject |         +           |            -               |          ?              |
    | ------- | ------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
    | He      | speaks English.     | doesn't speak English.     | Does he speak English?  |
    | She     | speaks English.     | doesn't speak English.     | Does she speak English? |
    | It      | eats dog food.      | doesn't eat dog food.      | Does it eat dog food?   |

📌 Short Answers

    | Question                | Positive       | Negative         |
    | ----------------------- | -------------- | ---------------- |
    | Does he work?           | Yes, he does.  | No, he doesn't.  |
    | Does she speak English? | Yes, she does. | No, she doesn't. |
    | Does it eat dog food?   | Yes, it does.  | No, it doesn't.  |
