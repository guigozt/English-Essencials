# Exercise 1: Present Simple (He, She, It & Plurals)

Complete as lacunas utilizando a forma correta dos verbos entre parênteses:

---

### Questões

1. I \_\_\_ *(not / live)* in Colombia.
2. She \_\_\_ *(like)* my friends.
3. He \_\_\_ *(not / study)* English.
4. It \_\_\_ *(not / eat)* dog food.
5. They \_\_\_ *(need)* a new car.

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas (Forma Declarativa / Negativa)</b></summary>

<br>

1. I **don't live** in Colombia. *(Eu não moro na Colômbia)*
2. She **likes** my friends. *(Ela gosta dos meus amigos — adiciona -s)*
3. He **doesn't study** English. *(Ele não estuda inglês — usa doesn't e o verbo volta à forma base)*
4. It **doesn't eat** dog food. *(Ele/Ela não come ração de cachorro)*
5. They **need** a new car. *(Eles precisam de um carro novo)*

</details>

<br>

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Bônus: Como ficariam essas frases como Perguntas?</b></summary>

<br>

1. **Do** I live in Colombia?
2. **Does** she like my friends?
3. **Does** he study English? *(ou negativa interrogativa: Doesn't he study English?)*
4. **Does** it eat dog food?
5. **Do** they need a new car?

</details>
