Complete the blanks using the correct form of the verb

    1.  I _ (not, live) in Colombia
    2.  She _ (like) my friends
    3.  He _ (not, study) English
    4.  It _ (not, eat) dog foods
    5.  They _ (need) a new car

Answers 1:

    1.  I don't live in Colombia
    2.  She likes my friends
    3.  He doesn't study English
    4.  It doesn't eat dog foods
    5.  They need a new car

Answers 2:

    1.  Do I live in Colombia?
    2.  Does She like my friends?
    3.  Doesn't He study English?
    4.  Does It not eat dog foods?
    5.  Do They need a new car?
