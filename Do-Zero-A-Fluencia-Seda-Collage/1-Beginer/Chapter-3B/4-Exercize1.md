# Exercise 1: Demonstratives (This, That, These, Those)

Complete as perguntas usando **this**, **these**, **that** ou **those** de acordo com a indicação de proximidade:

---

### Questões

1. What's \_\_\_? *(near / perto)*
2. Are \_\_\_ key rings? *(far / longe)*
3. Is \_\_\_ a pencil? *(far / longe)*
4. Are \_\_\_ T-shirts? *(near / perto)*
5. What are \_\_\_? *(near / perto)*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta</b></summary>

<br>

1. What's **this**? *(singular, perto)*
2. Are **those** key rings? *(plural, longe)*
3. Is **that** a pencil? *(singular, longe)*
4. Are **these** T-shirts? *(plural, perto)*
5. What are **these**? *(plural, perto)*

</details>
