Demonstratives (Demonstrativos)

->  Usamos essas palavras para indicar se algo está perto ou longe.

    Singular
    * This ->   Isso/Isto               (Perto)
    * That ->   Aquilo/Aquele/Aquela    (Longe)

1.  Situation

    William: "Excuse me. What's that?"
    Salesman: "It's a hat"
    William: "And... is this a bag?"
    Salesman: "No, it isn't. It's a T-shirt"

2.  Situation
    
    Plural:
    * These ->  Estes/Estas/Esses/Essas   (Perto)
    * Those ->  Aqueles/Aquelas           (Longe)

    William: "I'm sorry... What are these?"
    Salesman: "They're mugs"
    William: "And... Are those postcards?"
    Salesman: "No, they aren't. They're maps"
