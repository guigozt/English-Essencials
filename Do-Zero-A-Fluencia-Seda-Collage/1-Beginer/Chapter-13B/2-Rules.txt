Spelling rules for regular Verbs

work        |   worked      |   verb + ed
finish      |   finished    |   verb + ed
--------------------------------------------------------------
live        |   lived       |   verb + d
change      |   changed     |   verb + d
--------------------------------------------------------------
cry         |   cried       |   -y + ied
study       |   studied     |   -y + ied
--------------------------------------------------------------
stop        |   stopped     |   consonant + vowel + consonant
travel      |   travelled   |   2x final consonant + ed
