Write the sentences in the past simple:

    1- They work in a hospital.
    2- We live in Ireland.
    3- You talk a lot.
    4- He waits for the bus.
    5- I relax at the weekend.

    2.  We lived in Ireland
    3.  You talked a lot
    4.  He waited for a bus
