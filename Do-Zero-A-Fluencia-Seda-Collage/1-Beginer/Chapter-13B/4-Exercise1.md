# Exercise 1: Transform to Simple Past (Affirmative)

Reescreva as frases do presente para o **Past Simple** utilizando a regra correta dos verbos regulares:

---

### Questões

1. They work in a hospital. $\longrightarrow$
2. We live in Ireland. $\longrightarrow$
3. You talk a lot. $\longrightarrow$
4. He waits for the bus. $\longrightarrow$
5. I relax at the weekend. $\longrightarrow$

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

1. **They worked in a hospital.**  
   * *Verbo:* *work* + *-ed* $\rightarrow$ *worked*.

2. **We lived in Ireland.**  
   * *Verbo:* Termina em *-e* $\rightarrow$ adiciona apenas *-d* (*lived*).

3. **You talked a lot.**  
   * *Verbo:* *talk* + *-ed* $\rightarrow$ *talked*.

4. **He waited for the bus.**  
   * *Verbo:* *wait* + *-ed* $\rightarrow$ *waited*.

5. **I relaxed at the weekend.**  
   * *Verbo:* *relax* + *-ed* $\rightarrow$ *relaxed*.

</details>
