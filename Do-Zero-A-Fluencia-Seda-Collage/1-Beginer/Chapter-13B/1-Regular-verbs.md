# Past Simple: Regular Verbs (Verbos Regulares no Passado)

Os verbos regulares em inglês formam o passado com o sufixo **-ed** ou **-d**, mantendo a **mesma forma** para todas as pessoas (*I, you, he, she, it, we, they*).

---

### 1. Narrativa: Um Sonho de Intercâmbio

> *"I always **wanted** to study abroad. I **talked** to my father about it, and he **answered**: 'Go for it!'. My parents **helped** me with the money. First, I **wanted** to go to the UK, but it was very expensive and I didn't like the cold weather. So, I **decided** to go to Dublin and **started** classes in January 2010."*

* 🇧🇷 **Tradução:**  
  *"Eu sempre quis estudar no exterior. Falei com meu pai sobre isso e ele respondeu: 'Vai em frente!'. Meus pais me ajudaram com o dinheiro. Primeiro, eu queria ir para o Reino Unido, mas era muito caro e eu não gostava do clima frio. Então, decidi ir para Dublin e comecei as aulas em janeiro de 2010."*

---

### 2. Estrutura Gramatical

$$\begin{aligned}
\textbf{Afirmativa (+):} &\quad \mathbf{Sujeito} + \mathbf{verbo\text{-ed}} \\
\textbf{Negativa (-):} &\quad \mathbf{Sujeito} + \mathbf{didn't} + \mathbf{verbo\ base\ (sem\text{ -ed})} \\
\textbf{Interrogativa (?):} &\quad \mathbf{Did} + \mathbf{sujeito} + \mathbf{verbo\ base\ (sem\text{ -ed})}?
\end{aligned}$$

| Sujeito | Afirmativa (+) | Negativa (-) | Pergunta (?) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I** | *I **finished** the book.* | *I **didn't finish** the book.* | *Did you finish the book?* |
| **You** | *You **arrived** on time.* | *You **didn't arrive** on time.* | *Did you arrive on time?* |
| **He** | *He **wanted** to help.* | *He **didn't want** to help.* | *Did he want to help?* |
| **She** | *She **liked** the film.* | *She **didn't like** the film.* | *Did she like the film?* |
| **It** | *It **rained** yesterday.* | *It **didn't rain** yesterday.* | *Did it rain yesterday?* |
| **We** | *We **studied** hard.* | *We **didn't study** hard.* | *Did we study hard?* |
| **They** | *They **stopped** the car.* | *They **didn't stop** the car.* | *Did they stop the car?* |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Atenção ao auxiliar DID:**  
> Quando você usa **didn't** ou **did**, o verbo principal volta para a sua **forma base infinitiva**:  
> * ❌ *I didn't finished.* $\rightarrow$ ✅ **I didn't finish.**  
> * ❌ *Did you arrived?* $\rightarrow$ ✅ **Did you arrive?**
