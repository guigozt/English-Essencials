# Exercise 1: Object Pronouns

Complete as lacunas utilizando o pronome objeto adequado (**me**, **you**, **him**, **her**, **it**, **us**, **them**):

---

### Questões

1. I have a new phone, but I don't use \_\_\_.
2. Jim likes Sarah, but she doesn't like \_\_\_.
3. My children love music. I sing to \_\_\_ every day.
4. Emma is nice. I like \_\_\_ a lot.
5. You're great. I love \_\_\_.

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas</b></summary>

<br>

1. I have a new phone, but I don't use **it**. *(it refere-se a new phone)*
2. Jim likes Sarah, but she doesn't like **him**. *(him refere-se a Jim)*
3. My children love music. I sing to **them** every day. *(them refere-se a my children)*
4. Emma is nice. I like **her** a lot. *(her refere-se a Emma)*
5. You're great. I love **you**. *(you permanece idêntico)*
