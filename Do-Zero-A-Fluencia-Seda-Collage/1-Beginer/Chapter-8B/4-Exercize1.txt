Complete the blanks using the correct object pronoun

    1.  I have a new phone, but I don't use _
    2.  Jim likes Sarah, but she don't like _
    3.  My children love music. I sing to _ every day
    4.  Emma is nice. I like _ a lot
    5.  You're great. I love _

Answers:

    1.  I have a new phone, but I don't use it
    2.  Jim likes Sarah, but she don't like him
    3.  My children love music. I sing to them every day
    4.  Emma is nice. I like her a lot
    5.  You're great. I love you
