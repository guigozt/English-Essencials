# Exercise 3: Complete the Dialogue

Complete as lacunas do diálogo abaixo:

---

### Questão

- **John:** \_\_\_ you Paul?
- **Paul:** Yes, \_\_\_ \_\_\_. Very \_\_\_
- **John:** \_\_\_ to \_\_\_ you!

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Resposta</b></summary>

<br>

- **John:** **Are** you Paul?
- **Paul:** Yes, **I am**. Very **good**.
- **John:** **Nice** to **meet** you!
