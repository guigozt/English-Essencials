# Exercise 1: Present Simple or Present Continuous?

Preencha os espaços em branco com a forma correta do verbo entre parênteses, decidindo entre o **Present Simple** (para hábitos/rotinas) ou o **Present Continuous** (para ações no momento):

---

### Questões

1. My parents \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ dinner now. *(have)*
2. I never \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ at home on Mondays. *(stay)*
3. He \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ French today because he has an exam tomorrow. *(study)*
4. They always \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ at 8 o'clock. *(get up)*
5. I can't talk right now. I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. *(drive)*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

1. My parents **are having** dinner now.  
   * *Palavra-chave:* **now** (agora) $\rightarrow$ ação em andamento $\rightarrow$ Present Continuous (*are having*).

2. I never **stay** at home on Mondays.  
   * *Palavra-chave:* **never** (advérbio de frequência) / **on Mondays** $\rightarrow$ rotina habitual $\rightarrow$ Present Simple (*stay*).

3. He **is studying** French today. *(ou **He's studying**)*  
   * *Palavra-chave:* **today** (situação temporária do dia) $\rightarrow$ Present Continuous (*is studying*).

4. They always **get up** at 8.  
   * *Palavra-chave:* **always** (sempre) $\rightarrow$ rotina diária $\rightarrow$ Present Simple (*get up*).

5. I can't talk right now. I **am driving**. *(ou **I'm driving**)*  
   * *Palavra-chave:* **right now** / impossibilidade de falar no momento $\rightarrow$ Present Continuous (*am driving*).

</details>
