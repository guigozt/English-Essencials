# Exercise 2: Word Order in Questions

Coloque as palavras na ordem correta para formar perguntas:

---

### Questões

1. come bus to you by school did?
2. are Poland from friends your?
3. this time start does what class?
4. she born where was?
5. you live where do?

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Explicações</b></summary>

<br>

1. **Did you come to school by bus?**  
   * *Estrutura:* `Auxiliary (Did)` + `Subject (you)` + `Infinitive (come)` + `Complement (to school by bus)`.

2. **Are your friends from Poland?**  
   * *Estrutura:* `Be (Are)` + `Subject (your friends)` + `Complement (from Poland)`.

3. **What time does this class start?**  
   * *Estrutura:* `Question Word (What time)` + `Auxiliary (does)` + `Subject (this class)` + `Infinitive (start)`.

4. **Where was she born?**  
   * *Estrutura:* `Question Word (Where)` + `Be (was)` + `Subject (she)` + `born`.

5. **Where do you live?**  
   * *Estrutura:* `Question Word (Where)` + `Auxiliary (do)` + `Subject (you)` + `Infinitive (live)`.

</details>
