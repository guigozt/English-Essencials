# Exercise 1: Word Placement in Questions

Coloque a palavra ou expressão entre parênteses no lugar correto dentro de cada pergunta:

---

### Questões

1. Does finish at 10? *(the class)*
2. Where the bus station? *(is)*
3. How are you? *(old)*
4. Why you write to me? *(didn't)*
5. What this word mean? *(does)*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Explicações</b></summary>

<br>

1. Does **the class** finish at 10?  
   * *Estrutura:* `Auxiliary (Does)` + `Subject (the class)` + `Infinitive (finish)`.

2. Where **is** the bus station?  
   * *Estrutura:* `Question Word (Where)` + `Be (is)` + `Subject (the bus station)`.

3. How **old** are you?  
   * *Estrutura:* `How + adjective (old)` formam juntos a expressão interrogativa antes do verbo `be (are)`.

4. Why **didn't** you write to me?  
   * *Estrutura:* `Question Word (Why)` + `Auxiliary (didn't)` + `Subject (you)` + `Infinitive (write)`.

5. What **does** this word mean?  
   * *Estrutura:* `Question Word (What)` + `Auxiliary (does)` + `Subject (this word)` + `Infinitive (mean)`.
