Present continuous: be + ver + ing

    -   thing that are happening at the moment.
        e.g: I'm sending a message to Sarah

    -   longer periods of time(today, this week...)
        e.g: I'm working at home this week

    -   to describe a picture.
        e.g: In this picture the woman is standing near the window
