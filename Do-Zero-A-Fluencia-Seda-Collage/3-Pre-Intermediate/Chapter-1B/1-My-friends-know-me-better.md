# My Friends Know Me Better (Aparência e Personalidade)

Exemplos práticos de descrições físicas (**What does he/she look like?**) e de personalidade (**What is he/she like?**) em inglês.

---

### 1. Eva (por Laura)

| Pergunta | Descrição |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What does she look like?**<br>*(Aparência física)* | *"Eva and I are friends **since** we were children. Her dad is from Nigeria, and that's why she has that beautiful **dark skin** of hers. She's **quite tall and slim**, and she's got the most **expressive black eyes** ever. She has **dark shaved hair** and her teeth are perfectly **white**."* |
| **What's she like?**<br>*(Personalidade)* | *"I know her very well. When it comes to personality, Eva is **fairly friendly and sociable**. She goes out every Saturday with her friends. I think she has a good **sense of humor**... but she can be **too talkative** sometimes. The things she hates the most are **unkind and lazy** people."* |

---

### 2. Jacob (por Roy)

| Pergunta | Descrição |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What does he look like?**<br>*(Aparência física)* | *"Jacob is the coolest lad ever! We've been friends for 20 years. His father, Michael, is Irish and that's why he has a **red beard and red mustache**. He's got **big blue eyes** and **big ears**. I think he's getting a little **bald** on the back of his head... but don't tell him I told you that!"* |
| **What's he like?**<br>*(Personalidade)* | *"Well... Jacob is **clever and quite funny**, but only if he knows you well. He can be **extremely shy** with people he's not acquainted with. He's not an **extrovert**, that's for sure."* |

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Não confunda as duas perguntas:**
> * **What does he/she look like?** $\rightarrow$ Pergunta sobre a **aparência física** (*tall, slim, blue eyes, curly hair*).
> * **What is he/she like?** $\rightarrow$ Pergunta sobre a **personalidade** (*friendly, funny, shy, hard-working*).
