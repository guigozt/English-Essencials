# Exercise 1: Regular Verbs in Past Simple

Complete as frases abaixo conjugando os verbos entre parênteses no **Past Simple** (afirmativa ou negativa conforme indicado):

---

### Questões

a) They \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ many questions. *(not - ask)*  
b) She \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to David. *(not - talk)*  
c) He \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ in Scotland for many years. *(live)*  
d) The shop \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ at nine o'clock. *(close)*  
e) I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ a movie and then I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ a friend. *(watch / visit)*  

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

a) They **didn't ask** many questions.  
* *Justificativa:* Negativa no passado usa **didn't + verbo base** (*ask*).

b) She **didn't talk** to David.  
* *Justificativa:* Negativa no passado usa **didn't + verbo base** (*talk*).

c) He **lived** in Scotland for many years.  
* *Justificativa:* Verbo terminado em "e" (*live*) $\rightarrow$ apenas acrescenta **-d**.

d) The shop **closed** at nine o'clock.  
* *Justificativa:* Verbo terminado em "e" (*close*) $\rightarrow$ apenas acrescenta **-d**.

e) I **watched** a movie and then I **visited** a friend.  
* *Justificativa:* Ambos são regulares da regra geral $\rightarrow$ acrescenta-se **-ed** (*watch $\rightarrow$ watched*, *visit $\rightarrow$ visited*).

</details>
