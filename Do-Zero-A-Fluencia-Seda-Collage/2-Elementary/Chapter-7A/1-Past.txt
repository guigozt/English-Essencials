Past Simple – Regular Verbs

We use the Past Simple for finished actions in the past.

I watched a good film last night.

She called me yesterday.

We visited London in 2020.

Common time expressions:

| Expression        | Example                          |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| yesterday         | I called him yesterday.          |
| yesterday morning | She arrived yesterday morning.   |
| last night        | We watched a film last night.    |
| last week         | I worked a lot last week.        |
| last month        | They moved last month.           |
| last year         | He started university last year. |
| in 2010           | She moved to London in 2010.     |
| two days ago      | I saw him two days ago.          |

✅ Affirmative

For regular verbs, we normally add -ed.

| Subject | Past Simple |
| ------- | ----------- |
| I       | worked      |
| You     | worked      |
| He      | worked      |
| She     | worked      |
| It      | worked      |
| We      | worked      |
| They    | worked      |

worked

📌 The verb is the same for every person.
    Unlike the Present Simple, we don't add -s for he/she/it.

❌ Negative

Use:

Subject + didn't + base verb

I didn't work.

She didn't work.

They didn't work.

⚠️ Remember:

didn't already shows the past, so the main verb returns to its base form.

❓ Questions

Use:

Did + subject + base verb?

Did you work yesterday?

Did she call John?

Did they visit London?

Again, the main verb stays in the base form.

💬 Short Answers

| Question       | Positive       | Negative         |
| -------------- | -------------- | ---------------- |
| Did I work?    | Yes, I did.    | No, I didn't.    |
| Did he work?   | Yes, he did.   | No, he didn't.   |
| Did she work?  | Yes, she did.  | No, she didn't.  |
| Did we work?   | Yes, we did.   | No, we didn't.   |
| Did they work? | Yes, they did. | No, they didn't. |
