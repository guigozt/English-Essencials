Complete the sentences with the simple past of the verb in brackets:

    a) They ________________ many questions. (not-ask)
    b) She ________________ to David. (not-talk)
    c) He ________________ in Scotland for many years. (live)
    d) The shop ________________ at nine o'clock (close)
    e) I ________________ a movie and then I ________________ a friend. (watch / visit)

Answer:

    1.  They didn't ask many questions
    2.  She didnt' talk to David
    3.  He lived in Scotland for many years
    4.  The shop closed at nine o'clock
    5.  I watched a movie and then I visited a friend
