Countables / uncountables

-   Countables: (things you can count)
    Ex: an orange/ three oranges

-   Uncountables: (things you can't count)
    Ex: Butter, meat

Using a / an, some / any

                        Countable       |   Uncountable
    -----------------------------------------------------
    We need (+)         |   an apple        |   some butter
                        |   some apples     

    We don't need (-)   |   a tomate        |   any rice
                            any tomatoes

    Do we need (?)      |   any orange?     |   any sugar
                        |   any oranges?

Rules:

-   We use a / an with singular countables nouns = one
-   We use some in the (+) with plural countable nouns and uncountable nouns = not a exact number or quantity
-   We use any in the (-) and (?) with plural countables nouns and with ncountables nouns
