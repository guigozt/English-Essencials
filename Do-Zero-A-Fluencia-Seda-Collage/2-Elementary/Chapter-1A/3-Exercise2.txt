Complete the blanks with the correct personality adjectives

    1.  He can't talk in public. He's very _
    2.  Everyone's laughing at him because he's very _
    3.  She never smiles. She's _
    4.  She's always angry. She's _
    5.  They love sports. They're quite _

Answers:

    1.  He can't talk in public. He's very shy
    2.  Everyone's laughing at him because he's very funny
    3.  She never smiles. She's serious
    4.  She's always angry. She's moody
    5.  They love sports. They're quite active
