# Exercise 1: Describe Yourself (Descreva a Si Mesmo)

Pratique a autoapresentação em inglês preenchendo o modelo abaixo com seus dados pessoais e características de personalidade.

---

### Modelo de Apresentação

Preencha as lacunas com suas informações reais ou criativas:

> *"Hi, my name's \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. I was born in \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. So, I'm \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ years old. I'm a/an \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ and I work in a/an \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. I'm quite \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ and \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. I'm from \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, in Brazil, but I live in \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ now. My favorite food is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ and I love \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_."*

---

<summary><b>🔍 Ver Exemplo Resolvido</b></summary>

<br>

**Exemplo preenchido:**

> *"Hi, my name's **Guilherme**. I was born in **2004**. So, I'm **22** years old.  
> I'm a **student** and I work in a **hospital**. I'm quite **easy-going** and **funny**.  
> I'm from **São Paulo**, in Brazil, but I live in **Dublin** now.  
> My favorite food is **pizza** and I love **watching movies**."*

* 💡 **Dica gramatical:** Lembre-se de usar **"a"** antes de som consonantal (*a student, a hospital*) e **"an"** antes de som vocálico (*an actor, an office*).
