Brothers & Sisters

| Sentence                 | Meaning                |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| Jane is Jerry's sister.  | Jane é irmã do Jerry.  |
| Jerry is Jane's brother. | Jerry é irmão da Jane. |

📌 Together, they are siblings.

Sibling = irmão ou irmã (palavra neutra).

Examples:

I have two siblings.
She has one brother and one sister.
