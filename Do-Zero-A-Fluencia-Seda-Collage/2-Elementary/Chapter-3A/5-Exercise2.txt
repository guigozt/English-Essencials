Complete with the correct family member:

    a) My mother's husband is my ___________.
    b) My aunt's husband is my ___________.
    c) My father's brother is my ___________.
    d) My mother's sister is my ___________.
    e) My mother's mother is my ___________.
    f) My brother's son is my ___________.
    g) My sister's daughter's is my ___________.

Answer:

    a) My mother's husband is my father.
    b) My aunt's husband is my uncle.
    c) My father's brother is my uncle.
    d) My mother's sister is my aunt.
    e) My mother's mother is my grandma.
    f) My brother's son is my nephew.
    g) My sister's daughter's is my niece.
