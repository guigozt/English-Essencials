👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Members & Possession

We use 's to show:

👨‍👩‍👧 Family relationships
🎒 Possession (who owns something)

Examples:

John's father
Sarah's book
Maria's car
📸 Talking About Family Members
Example 1

Who's that woman next to Justin Bieber?

She's Justin Bieber's mother.

📖 Vocabulary

next to = ao lado de
mother = mãe
Example 2

Who's that man next to Britney Spears?

He's Britney Spears' father.

📌 Como Spears já termina em s, também é comum escrever:

Britney Spears's father (mais comum no inglês americano)

ou

Britney Spears' father (mais comum no inglês britânico)

Example 3

Who's that man holding Jack and William?

He's Jack and William's grandfather.

📖 Vocabulary

hold = segurar
grandfather (grandpa) = avô

📌 Como Jack e William têm o mesmo avô, usamos apenas um 's no último nome.

Example 4

Who's that woman in front of Jerry?

She's Jerry's grandmother.

📖 Vocabulary

in front of = em frente a
grandmother (grandma) = avó
Example 5

Who's that woman carrying Dave on her back?

She's Dave's aunt.

📖 Vocabulary

carry = carregar
on her back = nas costas
aunt = tia
Example 6

Who's that man sitting next to Will?

He's Will's uncle.

📖 Vocabulary

sit = sentar
sitting = sentado
uncle = tio
Example 7

Whose car is it?

It's Ronaldo's car.

It's Ronaldo's.

📌 Se o objeto já foi mencionado, podemos omiti-lo.

Examples:

It's Sarah's.
It's John's.
It's mine.
Example 8

Whose mug is it?

It's Maria's mug.

It's Maria's.
