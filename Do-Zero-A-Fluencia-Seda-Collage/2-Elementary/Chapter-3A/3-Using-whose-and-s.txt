Using 'whose' and 's

    1.  's
    -   We use 's to talk about family and possessions:
    -   He's Jane's uncle

    2.  s'
    -   We use s' NOT 's with regular plural nouns:
    -   They're my parents' friends.

    3.  whose
    -   We use whose to ask about possessions:
    -   Whose postacard is it?  /   Whose is it postcard?

    Attention!!
    -   Whose and Who's have the same sound
