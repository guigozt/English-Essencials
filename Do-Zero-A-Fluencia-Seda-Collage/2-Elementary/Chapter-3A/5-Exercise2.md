# Exercise 2: Family Relationships Logic

Complete as frases deduzindo o grau de parentesco correto:

---

### Questões

a) My mother's husband is my \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.  
b) My aunt's husband is my \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.  
c) My father's brother is my \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.  
d) My mother's sister is my \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.  
e) My mother's mother is my \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.  
f) My brother's son is my \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.  
g) My sister's daughter is my \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.  

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Tradução</b></summary>

<br>

a) My mother's husband is my **father** *(pai / ou stepfather se for padrasto)*.  
b) My aunt's husband is my **uncle** *(tio)*.  
c) My father's brother is my **uncle** *(tio)*.  
d) My mother's sister is my **aunt** *(tia)*.  
e) My mother's mother is my **grandmother** *(ou grandma - avó)*.  
f) My brother's son is my **nephew** *(sobrinho)*.  
g) My sister's daughter is my **niece** *(sobrinha)*.  

</details>
