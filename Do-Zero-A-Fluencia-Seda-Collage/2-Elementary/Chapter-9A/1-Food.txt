# 🍎 Food — Countable & Uncountable Nouns

## 1. Countable vs. Uncountable

### Countable nouns — coisas que podemos contar

Podemos usar números diretamente:

* 1 biscuit → 2 biscuits
* 1 apple → 3 apples
* 1 sweet → 5 sweets
* 1 egg → 6 eggs

👉 Geralmente possuem forma **singular e plural**.

**Example:**

> How many biscuits are there in the jar?
> There are **a few biscuits**.

---

### Uncountable nouns — coisas que não contamos individualmente

Normalmente não usamos números diretamente com essas palavras:

* milk
* water
* rice
* salt
* sugar
* bread
* cheese
* coffee

❌ **two milks**
✅ **two bottles of milk**

👉 Normalmente são tratados como **singular**.

**Example:**

> How much milk is there in the bottle?
> There is **a little milk**.

---

# 2. How much × How many

### 🥛 How much → Uncountable

Usamos **How much** para perguntar sobre quantidade de coisas incontáveis.

> How much **milk** is there?
> How much **water** do you drink?
> How much **salt** do you eat?

### 🍪 How many → Countable

Usamos **How many** para perguntar sobre coisas contáveis no plural.

> How many **biscuits** are there?
> How many **eggs** do you need?
> How many **apples** do you eat?

### 📌 Regra rápida

**How much + uncountable**

> How much **rice**?

**How many + plural countable**

> How many **biscuits**?

---

# 3. Quantifiers

Quantifiers são palavras que indicam **quantidade**.

## 🥛 Uncountable nouns

| Quantifier         | Meaning   | Example                          |
| ------------------ | --------- | -------------------------------- |
| **A lot of**       | muito(a)  | I drink **a lot of milk**.       |
| **Quite a lot of** | bastante  | I drink **quite a lot of milk**. |
| **A little**       | um pouco  | I drink **a little milk**.       |
| **Not much**       | não muito | I don't drink **much milk**.     |
| **None / not any** | nenhum(a) | I don't drink **any milk**.      |

### Question:

> **How much milk do you drink?**

Possible answers:

> **A lot.**
> **Quite a lot.**
> **A little.**
> **Not much.**
> **None.**

### Full answers:

> I drink **a lot of milk**.
> I drink **quite a lot of milk**.
> I drink **a little milk**.
> I don't drink **much milk**.
> I don't drink **any milk**.

---

# 4. Countable nouns

| Quantifier         | Meaning          | Example                            |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **A lot of**       | muitos(as)       | I eat **a lot of biscuits**.       |
| **Quite a lot of** | bastante         | I eat **quite a lot of biscuits**. |
| **A few**          | alguns / algumas | I eat **a few biscuits**.          |
| **Not many**       | não muitos(as)   | I don't eat **many biscuits**.     |
| **None / not any** | nenhum(a)        | I don't eat **any biscuits**.      |

### Question:

> **How many biscuits do you eat?**

Possible answers:

> **A lot.**
> **Quite a lot.**
> **A few.**
> **Not many.**
> **None.**

### Full answers:

> I eat **a lot of biscuits**.
> I eat **quite a lot of biscuits**.
> I eat **a few biscuits**.
> I don't eat **many biscuits**.
> I don't eat **any biscuits**.

---

# 5. A little × A few

Essa diferença é importante:

### **A little → uncountable**

> I have **a little water**.
> I eat **a little rice**.

= Tenho/como **um pouco de...**

### **A few → countable plural**

> I have **a few apples**.
> I eat **a few biscuits**.

= Tenho/como **alguns/algumas...**

📌 Pense assim:

**A little → quantidade de algo que não contamos individualmente**

**A few → algumas unidades que podemos contar**

# 🧠 Quick Review

### COUNTABLE

Podemos contar:

> one apple → two apples

Usamos:

**How many?**

> How many apples?

E podemos usar:

> a few apples
> many apples
> a lot of apples
> not many apples
> any apples

---

### UNCOUNTABLE

Não contamos individualmente:

> water, milk, rice, salt, sugar

Usamos:

**How much?**

> How much water?

E podemos usar:

> a little water
> much water
> a lot of water
> not much water
> any water

---

### ⭐ Regra para lembrar

> **HOW MANY → COUNTABLE PLURAL** 🍪🍪🍪
> **HOW MUCH → UNCOUNTABLE** 🥛💧
