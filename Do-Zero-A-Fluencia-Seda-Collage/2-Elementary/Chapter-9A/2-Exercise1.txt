Exercise 1 - Complete the sentences with "how much" or "how many":

    1.  _ tins of tuna do you have?
    2.  _ olive oil I need?
    3.  _ strawberry juice is there in the cartoon?
    4.  _ bottles of milk did you buy?
    5.  _ rice do you want?

Answer:

    1.  How many tins of tuna do they have?
    2.  How much olive oil do I need?
    3.  How much strawberry juice is there in the cartoon?
    4.  How much bottles of milk did you buy?
    5.  How much rice do you want?
