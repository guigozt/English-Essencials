Can / Can't + Infinitve can be used as:

    1.  I know how to / I don't know how to...
    -   I can sing, but I can't dance

    2.  It's possible / It's not possible:
    -   I can come on Tuesday, but I can't come on Friday

    3.  It's permitted / It's not permitted:
    -   You can park here, but You can't park there

    4.  Please do it (Can you) / Is it OK if i do it?:
    -   Can you help me? / Can i open the window?
