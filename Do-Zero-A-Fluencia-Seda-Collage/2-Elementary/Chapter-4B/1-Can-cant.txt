💬 Can / Can't

The modal verb can is used to talk about:

✅ Ability (skills)
✅ Possibility
✅ Permission
✅ Requests
✅ Asking for permission

📌 Can is a modal verb, so it is always followed by the base form (infinitive without "to").

Structure

| Positive                 | Negative                   | Question                  |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Subject + **can** + verb | Subject + **can't** + verb | **Can** + subject + verb? |

Examples:

I can swim.
She can't drive.
Can you help me?

🎤 Example 1 – Ability

Jenny: "I can sing quite well, I guess."

Karen: "No, you can't! You don't know how to sing at all."

Meaning

Can expresses ability (what someone knows how to do).

📌 Vocabulary

| Expression | Meaning                        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------ |
| sing       | cantar                         |
| quite well | muito bem / razoavelmente bem  |
| I guess    | eu acho                        |
| at all     | de jeito nenhum / nem um pouco |

⏰ Example 2 – Possibility

Alex: "I can be here at seven o'clock if needed."

Sarah: "Are you sure?"

Alex: "Yes, of course. It's possible for me."

Meaning

Here, can means it is possible.

Examples:

I can come tomorrow.
We can't meet today.
She can finish the project this week.

🤫 Example 3 – Asking for Permission

Bob: "Come here! Can I tell you a secret?"

Nath: "No, you can't. I'm terrible at keeping secrets. Talk to Monica!"

Meaning

Can I... ?

= Asking permission.

Examples:

Can I sit here?
Can I use your phone?
Can I open the window?

Short Answers

| Question       | Positive       | Negative        |
| -------------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| Can you swim?  | Yes, I can.    | No, I can't.    |
| Can she cook?  | Yes, she can.  | No, she can't.  |
| Can they help? | Yes, they can. | No, they can't. |
