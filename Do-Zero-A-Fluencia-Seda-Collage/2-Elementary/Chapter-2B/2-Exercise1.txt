Complete the verb phrases:

    a) They _______ Biology. (+)
    b) Peter _______ in a bar. (-)
    c) Jenny _______ books in German. (+)
    d) Julia _______ got three children (-)
    e) Paul _______ glasses (+)
    f) I ________ to music on the bus (+)
    g) My friend ________ rugby in Japan. (-)

Answers:

    1.  They study Biology.
    2.  Peter doesn't work in a bar
    3.  Jenny reads books in German
    4.  Julia hasn't got three children
    5.  Paul wears glasses
    6.  I listen to music on the bus
    7.  My friend doesn't play rugby in Japan
