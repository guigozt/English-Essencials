# Everyday Dialogues: Present Perfect & Life Experiences

Diálogos cotidianos que demonstram o uso natural do **Present Perfect** para falar de experiências de vida, eventos recentes e notícias, alternando naturalmente com o **Past Simple** quando o momento no passado é especificado.

---

### 1. 🎬 Movies & Books

* **Jane:** *"Have you seen The Lord of the Rings? They say it's a very good movie."*
* **Jim:** *"No, I haven't, but I've read the book."*
* **Jane:** *"Did you like it?"*
* **Jim:** *"I loved it!"*

> [!NOTE]
> * Pergunta de experiência sem momento definido: **Have you seen...?**
> * Resposta curta no Present Perfect: **No, I haven't.**
> * Ao focar no momento e na reação passada: muda-se para o Past Simple (**Did you like it? — I loved it!**).

---

### 2. 📝 Homework

* **Paula:** *"Have you done your homework?"*
* **Maggie:** *"Yes, I have. I did it yesterday."*

> [!TIP]
> * Observe a transição: **Have you done...?** (a tarefa está pronta agora?) $\rightarrow$ **Yes, I have.** Mas ao especificar o tempo (**yesterday**), usa-se o Past Simple: **I did it yesterday.**

---

### 3. ❤️ Big News & Recent Events

* **Larry:** *"I guess Terry's fallen in love with an Italian girl!"*
* **William:** *"Really? That's so cool!"*
* **Larry:** *"Yeap... He's just told me that."*

> [!NOTE]
> * Contração de *has*: **Terry's fallen** = *Terry has fallen*.
> * Uso de **just** para eventos que acabaram de acontecer: **He's just told me**.

---

### 4. 🌟 Celebrity Encounters

* **Jerry:** *"Has your brother ever taken a photo with an actor?"*
* **Earl:** *"Yes, he has. He's taken a photo with Sylvester Stallone."*
* **Jerry:** *"And yourself?"*
* **Earl:** *"No, I have never met a famous person."*

> [!NOTE]
> * **ever** = em algum momento da vida (perguntas).
> * **never** = nunca (resposta com valor negativo, sem precisar de *not*).

---

### 5. ⚠️ Work Deadlines

* **John:** *"Have you finished your report?"*
* **Paul:** *"No, I haven't."*
* **John:** *"But... the coordinator has arrived!"*
* **Paul:** *"Oh, no! I'm so busted!"*

> [!NOTE]
> * Ação com impacto no presente: **the coordinator has arrived** (ele está aqui agora).
> * *I'm so busted!* = Estou frito / me dei mal!

---

### 6. ✈️ Travel & Experiences Abroad

* **John:** *"Have you ever visited Paris?"*
* **Paul:** *"I've never been abroad before, but my parents have travelled to France twice."*

> [!NOTE]
> * Diferença cultural e gramatical: **been to** indica que a pessoa foi e voltou (experiência).
> * Frequência de experiências de vida: **twice** (duas vezes).
