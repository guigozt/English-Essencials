# Exercise 2: Present Perfect Negative & Question Forms

Reescreva as frases no Present Perfect de acordo com a forma indicada entre parênteses: **negativa (-)** ou **interrogativa (?)**.

---

### Questões

1. He has broken his arm. `(-)`
   * $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

2. She has gone to the beach. `(-)`
   * $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

3. I have fallen off my bike. `(-)`
   * $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

4. They have read the newspaper. `(?)`
   * $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

5. She has bought a new car. `(?)`
   * $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

1. **He hasn't broken his arm.** *(ou: He has not broken his arm)*
   * *Regra:* Acrescenta-se *not* ao auxiliar *has* $\rightarrow$ **hasn't**.
2. **She hasn't gone to the beach.** *(ou: She has not gone to the beach)*
   * *Regra:* Negativa de *has* $\rightarrow$ **hasn't**.
3. **I haven't fallen off my bike.** *(ou: I have not fallen off my bike)*
   * *Regra:* Acrescenta-se *not* ao auxiliar *have* $\rightarrow$ **haven't**.
4. **Have they read the newspaper?**
   * *Regra:* Inverte-se o auxiliar antes do sujeito: **Have + they + read...?**
5. **Has she bought a new car?**
   * *Regra:* Inverte-se o auxiliar antes do sujeito: **Has + she + bought...?**

</details>
