# Exercise 2: Telling a Short Story in the Past (Produção Pessoal)

Exercício guiado de produção de narrativa no passado (*storytelling*), respondendo às perguntas de referência.

---

### ❓ Perguntas Guia

1. **When was it?** *(Quando foi?)*
2. **Where were you?** *(Onde você estava?)*
3. **Who were you with?** *(Com quem você estava?)*
4. **What did you wear?** *(O que você vestiu?)*
5. **Where did you go?** *(Onde vocês foram?)*
6. **What did you do?** *(O que vocês fizeram?)*
7. **What was the weather like?** *(Como estava o clima?)*
8. **What time did you get home?** *(A que horas você chegou em casa?)*

---

### ✍️ Modelo de História Pronta (Revisada & Comentada)

> *"It was in 2024. I went to meet my girlfriend at the station. My girlfriend and my brother-in-law were with me. I wore a white shirt and black trousers, as usual. We went to the cinema in the city centre and watched 'Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse'. The weather was cold and cloudy in the evening. In the end, we got home late at night."*

* 🇧🇷 **Tradução:**
  Foi em 2024. Fui encontrar minha namorada na estação. Minha namorada e meu cunhado estavam comigo. Vesti uma camisa branca e calças pretas, como de costume. Fomos ao cinema no centro da cidade e assistimos a "Homem-Aranha no Aranhaverso". O tempo estava frio e nublado no início da noite. No fim das contas, chegamos em casa tarde da noite.

---

### 💡 Vocabulário Útil para Narrativas Pessoais

| Pergunta / Foco | Expressão / Conector | Exemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tempo / Época** | *It was two years ago / last summer / in 2024* | *It was last Christmas.* |
| **Companhia** | *I was on my own / with my family / with my colleagues* | *I was with my best friend.* |
| **Roupas** | *I wore a jacket / jeans / sneakers / a dress* | *I wore casual clothes.* |
| **Ações** | *We decided to go... / We spent time... / We tried...* | *We had dinner at an Italian place.* |
| **Clima** | *It was sunny / rainy / freezing / windy* | *It was a warm summer night.* |
| **Desfecho** | *Finally / In the end / Eventually* | *We got home around midnight.* |
