Past Simple – Irregular Verbs

We use the Past Simple for finished actions in the past.

I went to Spain last month.

She met her friends yesterday.

We had dinner at 8 o'clock.

What are irregular verbs?

Regular verbs usually add -ed:

walk → walked
work → worked
play → played

Irregular verbs do not follow the -ed rule:

go → went
meet → met
have → had
see → saw
wear → wore

There is no single rule for irregular verbs, so you need to learn their past forms individually.

Affirmative (+)

Use the irregular past form.

| Subject | Example    |
| ------- | ---------- |
| I       | went home. |
| He      | went home. |
| She     | went home. |
| We      | went home. |
| They    | went home. |

Negative (-)

Use:

subject + didn't + infinitive/base verb

This is very important:

She went home.

becomes:

She didn't go home.

NOT:

❌ She didn't went home.

The didn't already carries the past, so the main verb returns to its base form.

| Affirmative | Negative    |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| went        | didn't go   |
| saw         | didn't see  |
| met         | didn't meet |
| had         | didn't have |
| wore        | didn't wear |
| bought      | didn't buy  |

Questions (?)

Use:

Did + subject + base verb?

Did you go to the cinema?

Did she meet John?

Did they have dinner?

Again, the verb goes back to the infinitive/base form.

❌ Did you went?

✅ Did you go?

❌ Did she saw him?

✅ Did she see him?

Short Answers

| Question         | Yes            | No               |
| ---------------- | -------------- | ---------------- |
| Did you go?      | Yes, I did.    | No, I didn't.    |
| Did he go?       | Yes, he did.   | No, he didn't.   |
| Did she see him? | Yes, she did.  | No, she didn't.  |
| Did they meet?   | Yes, they did. | No, they didn't. |

Could / Couldn't

Could is the past form of can when talking about ability or possibility in the past.

I can swim. → Present

I could swim when I was five. → Past

Negative:

I couldn't swim when I was five.

Questions

Could you swim when you were five?

Could she speak English?

We do not use did with could:

❌ Did you can swim?

❌ I didn't can swim.

✅ Could you swim?

✅ I couldn't swim.
