Complete the sentences with the past form of the verbs in brackets:

    a) Last week, I ______________ (go) to Berlin with some friends.
    b) She ______________ (wear) a green dress yesterday.
    c) They ______________ (not - get) home at midnight.
    d) He ______________ (lose) his mobile phone.
    e) I ______________ (not-leave) work early.

Answers:

    1.  Last week, I went to Berin with some friends
    2.  She wore a green dress yesterday
    3.  They didn't get home at midnight
    4.  He lost his mobile phone
    5.  I didn't leave work early
