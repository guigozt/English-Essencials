# Exercise 1: Past Simple of Irregular Verbs

Complete as frases abaixo colocando os verbos entre parênteses no **Past Simple** (afirmativa ou negativa conforme indicado):

---

### Questões

a) Last week, I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to Berlin with some friends. *(go)*  
b) She \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ a green dress yesterday. *(wear)*  
c) They \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ home at midnight. *(not - get)*  
d) He \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ his mobile phone. *(lose)*  
e) I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ work early. *(not - leave)*  

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

a) Last week, I **went** to Berlin with some friends.  
* *Justificativa:* *Go* é irregular; sua forma afirmativa no passado é **went**.

b) She **wore** a green dress yesterday.  
* *Justificativa:* *Wear* é irregular; sua forma afirmativa no passado é **wore**.

c) They **didn't get** home at midnight.  
* *Justificativa:* Negativa no passado usa **didn't + verbo na forma base** (*get*).

d) He **lost** his mobile phone.  
* *Justificativa:* *Lose* é irregular; sua forma afirmativa no passado é **lost**.

e) I **didn't leave** work early.  
* *Justificativa:* Negativa no passado usa **didn't + verbo na forma base** (*leave*).
