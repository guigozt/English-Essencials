# Exercise 1: There is / was / are / were Transformation

Reescreva as frases abaixo transformando-as na forma indicada entre parênteses: **afirmativa $(+)$**, **negativa $(-)$** ou **interrogativa $(?)$**, prestando atenção à concordância e ao tempo verbal (presente ou passado):

---

### Questões

1. There's a dishwasher in the kitchen. $(?)$ $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
2. There were some pictures on the wall. $(-)$ $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
3. Is there a carpet on the floor? $(+)$ $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
4. There weren't any chairs in the garden. $(+)$ $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
5. There was a carpet on the floor. $(-)$ $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

1. **Is there a dishwasher in the kitchen?**
   * *Justificativa:* Presente interrogativo no singular: inverte-se *There is* para *Is there*.

2. **There weren't any pictures on the wall.**
   * *Justificativa:* Passado negativo no plural: *were not (weren't)* e troca-se *some* por *any*.

3. **There's a carpet on the floor.** *(ou There is a carpet on the floor)*
   * *Justificativa:* Presente afirmativo no singular.

4. **There were some chairs in the garden.**
   * *Justificativa:* Passado afirmativo no plural: *were* e troca-se *any* por *some*.

5. **There wasn't a carpet on the floor.**
   * *Justificativa:* Passado negativo no singular: *was not (wasn't)*.
