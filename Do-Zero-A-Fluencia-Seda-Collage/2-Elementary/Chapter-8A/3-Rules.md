# There is / There are & There was / There were

Usamos a estrutura **there + be** para indicar a **existência ou presença** de algo ou alguém em um determinado local.

* **Present Simple:** *There is* (singular) / *There are* (plural) $\rightarrow$ *Há / Tem / Existe(m)*
* **Past Simple:** *There was* (singular) / *There were* (plural) $\rightarrow$ *Havia / Tinha / Existia(m)*

---

### 1. Present Simple: There is / There are

| Tipo de Frase | Singular | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Afirmativa (+)** | **There's** a vase on the shelf. | **There are** some pictures on the wall. |
| **Negativa (-)** | **There isn't** a swimming pool. | **There aren't** any plants in the room. |
| **Interrogativa (?)** | **Is there** a bathroom downstairs? | **Are there** any neighbours with children? |
| **Short Answers** | • *Yes, there is.*<br>• *No, there isn't.* | • *Yes, there are.*<br>• *No, there aren't.* |

---

### 2. Past Simple: There was / There were

| Tipo de Frase | Singular | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Afirmativa (+)** | **There was** a vase on the shelf. | **There were** some pictures on the wall. |
| **Negativa (-)** | **There wasn't** a swimming pool. | **There weren't** any plants in the room. |
| **Interrogativa (?)** | **Was there** a bathroom downstairs? | **Were there** any neighbours with children? |
| **Short Answers** | • *Yes, there was.*<br>• *No, there wasn't.* | • *Yes, there were.*<br>• *No, there weren't.* |

---

### 3. Regras Gramaticais Importantes

1. **Contrações:**
   * No presente afirmativo: *There is* frequentemente contrai para **There's**.
   * *There are* geralmente **não** se contrai na escrita formal.
   * Nas negativas: *There isn't / aren't* e *There wasn't / weren't*.

2. **Listas de Itens:**
   * Ao enumerar vários itens em uma lista, o verbo concorda com o **primeiro item** mencionado:
     * *There **is** a lamp and two chairs.* (singular porque "a lamp" é singular)
     * *There **are** two chairs and a lamp.* (plural porque "two chairs" é plural)

3. **Combinação com a/an, some e any:**
   * Usamos **a / an** com substantivos contáveis no singular:
     * *There is **a** book.* / *There was **an** armchair.*
   * Usamos **some** em frases afirmativas com substantivos no plural ou incontáveis:
     * *There are **some** books on the table.*
     * *There was **some** milk in the fridge.*
   * Usamos **any** em frases negativas e interrogativas:
     * *There aren't **any** magazines.* / *There weren't **any** glasses.*
     * *Are there **any** chairs?* / *Were there **any** problems?*
