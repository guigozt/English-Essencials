📚 Present Simple × Present Continuous

Both tenses talk about the present, but they are used in different situations.

| **Present Simple**       | **Present Continuous**                     |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Habits and routines      | Actions happening now                      |
| Facts and general truths | Temporary actions                          |
| Permanent situations     | Things happening around the present moment |

📊 Comparison

| Present Simple                    | Present Continuous                                |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| I work at home in the winter.     | I'm working at home because it's snowing outside. |
| It rains a lot in Dublin.         | Look! It's raining outside.                       |
| What do you usually wear to work? | What are you wearing today?                       |
| She studies every day.            | She is studying now.                              |
| They play football on Saturdays.  | They are playing football at the moment.          |
