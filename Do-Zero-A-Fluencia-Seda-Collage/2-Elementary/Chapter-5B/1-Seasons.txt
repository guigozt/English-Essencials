🌦️ The Seasons

There are four seasons in a year:

🌸 Spring
☀️ Summer
🍂 Autumn (Fall)
❄️ Winter

Each season has different weather, temperatures, and activities.

☀️ Summer
Weather
The days are sunny and hot.
It usually doesn't rain much.
People spend more time outdoors.
Common Activities
Go for a walk.
Go to the park.
Go to the beach.
Have a picnic.
Example Text

In the summertime, the days are usually sunny and hot. People often go for a walk in the park to enjoy the good weather. This photograph shows a couple strolling around Phoenix Park, on the north side of Dublin.

🍂 Autumn (Fall)

📌 Autumn is used in British English.

📌 Fall is used in American English.

Weather
The weather becomes cooler.
The days are often cloudy and rainy.
The mornings can be foggy.
Leaves fall from the trees.
Common Activities
Wear a raincoat.
Drink hot coffee.
Walk through the park.
Example Text

During autumn, the days become cloudy and rainy. People usually wear raincoats when they go outside. The mornings can be quite foggy because the temperature starts to decrease. This photograph shows people walking through the streets of Dublin, trying to see through the fog.

Useful Vocabulary

| Word          | Meaning       |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| cloudy        | nublado       |
| rainy         | chuvoso       |
| foggy         | com neblina   |
| raincoat      | capa de chuva |
| leaf (leaves) | folha         |
| decrease      | diminuir      |

Example Sentences
It is cloudy today.
I need a raincoat because it's raining.
The mornings are very foggy in autumn.

❄️ Winter
Weather
Winter is the coldest season.
The days are cold and windy.
Sometimes it snows.
The temperature can go below 0°C.
Common Activities
Wear warm clothes.
Drink hot chocolate.
Build a snowman.
Play in the snow.
Example Text

Winter is the coldest season of the year. The days are cold and windy. When the temperature gets very low, it snows. Children love playing in the snow! This photograph shows four children playing on Parnell Street, in Dublin city centre.

Useful Vocabulary

| Word    | Meaning        |
| ------- | -------------- |
| cold    | frio           |
| windy   | ventando       |
| snow    | neve           |
| snowman | boneco de neve |
| gloves  | luvas          |
| scarf   | cachecol       |
| boots   | botas          |

Example Sentences
It's very cold today.
I wear a coat in winter.
The children are playing in the snow.

🌸 Spring
Weather
The weather starts to get warmer.
Flowers begin to bloom.
Trees become green again.
The days become longer.
Common Activities
Plant trees and flowers.
Go for a walk.
Enjoy the parks.
Example Text

In spring, the weather starts to get warm again. It is a great season for planting trees in your garden. The city becomes colorful because there are plenty of flowers everywhere. This photograph shows Dubliners going to church on Sunday morning.

Useful Vocabulary

| Word     | Meaning   |
| -------- | --------- |
| spring   | primavera |
| flower   | flor      |
| bloom    | florescer |
| colorful | colorido  |
| garden   | jardim    |
| plant    | plantar   |

Example Sentences
The flowers bloom in spring.
The weather is warm and pleasant.
We plant flowers in the garden.

| ❄️ Winter | Cold, windy, snowy   | Wear warm clothes, play in the snow |
