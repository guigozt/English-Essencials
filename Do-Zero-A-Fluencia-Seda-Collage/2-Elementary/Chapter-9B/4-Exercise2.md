# Exercise 2: Writing High Numbers

Escreva por extenso em inglês os números a seguir:

---

### Questões

1. **120** $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
2. **245,000** $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
3. **1,000,001** $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
4. **504,282** $\rightarrow$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Dicas de Grafia</b></summary>

<br>

1. **120** $\rightarrow$ **one hundred and twenty** *(ou "a hundred and twenty")*
2. **245,000** $\rightarrow$ **two hundred and forty-five thousand** *(lembre-se do hífen em "forty-five" e de "thousand" sem "s")*
3. **1,000,001** $\rightarrow$ **one million and one** *(ou "a million and one")*
4. **504,282** $\rightarrow$ **five hundred and four thousand, two hundred and eighty-two**
