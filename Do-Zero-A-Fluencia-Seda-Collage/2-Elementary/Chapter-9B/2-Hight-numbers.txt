# High Numbers: 20–100

| Number          | Numeral | Example                        |
| --------------- | ------: | ------------------------------ |
| Twenty          |      20 | 21 → **twenty-one**            |
| Thirty          |      30 | 32 → **thirty-two**            |
| Forty           |      40 | 43 → **forty-three**           |
| Fifty           |      50 | 54 → **fifty-four**            |
| Sixty           |      60 | 65 → **sixty-five**            |
| Seventy         |      70 | 76 → **seventy-six**           |
| Eighty          |      80 | 87 → **eighty-seven**          |
| Ninety          |      90 | 98 → **ninety-eight**          |
| One / a hundred |     100 | 109 → **one hundred and nine** |

### 📌 Important

Between tens and units, we use a **hyphen (-)**:

> 21 → twenty-**one**
> 47 → forty-**seven**
> 89 → eighty-**nine**

# 🧠 Number Structure

Think of large numbers in groups of three:

**1 | 234 | 567**

→ **one million | two hundred and thirty-four thousand | five hundred and sixty-seven**

### The main units:

|       Numeral | English  |
| ------------: | -------- |
|           100 | hundred  |
|         1,000 | thousand |
|     1,000,000 | million  |
| 1,000,000,000 | billion  |

---

# ⭐ Important Rules

### 1. Hundred, thousand and million don't get plural **-s** after a number

❌ two millions
❌ five thousands
❌ three hundreds

✅ two **million**
✅ five **thousand**
✅ three **hundred**

But **hundreds/thousands/millions** can be used when the exact number is not specified:

> **hundreds of people**
> **thousands of dollars**
> **millions of people**

---

### 2. "And"

In British English, **and** is commonly used before the final tens/units:

> 105 → **one hundred and five**
> 250 → **two hundred and fifty**
> 1,205 → **one thousand two hundred and five**

For larger numbers:

> 1,250 → **one thousand two hundred and fifty**

---

### 3. Hyphen

Use a hyphen between tens and units:

> 21 → **twenty-one**
> 56 → **fifty-six**
> 342 → **three hundred and forty-two**
> 2,567 → **two thousand five hundred and sixty-seven**
