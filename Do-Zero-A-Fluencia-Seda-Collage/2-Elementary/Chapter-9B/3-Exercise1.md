# Exercise 1: Comparative Adjectives in Context

Complete as lacunas utilizando a forma comparativa adequada do adjetivo indicado entre parênteses:

---

### Questões

1. I'm 95 kg. My wife is 78 kg. I'm \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ than her. *(heavy)*
2. I'm 1.98 m. My brother is 1.90 m. I'm \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ than him. *(tall)*
3. Right now, it's 38°C in Recife and 22°C in São Paulo. Recife is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ than São Paulo. *(hot)*
4. A pack of cigarettes is €10. A box of chocolates is €6. A box of chocolates is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ than a pack of cigarettes. *(cheap)*

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Comentários</b></summary>

<br>

1. I'm **heavier** than her.
   * *Regra:* Consoante + "y" (*heavy*) $\rightarrow$ substitui o "y" por *-ier*.

2. I'm **taller** than him.
   * *Regra:* Adjetivo curto regular (*tall*) $\rightarrow$ acrescenta *-er*.

3. Recife is **hotter** than São Paulo.
   * *Regra:* CVC — Consoante + Vogal + Consoante (*hot*) $\rightarrow$ dobra a consoante final + *-er*.

4. A box of chocolates is **cheaper** than a pack of cigarettes.
   * *Regra:* Adjetivo curto regular (*cheap*) $\rightarrow$ acrescenta *-er*.
