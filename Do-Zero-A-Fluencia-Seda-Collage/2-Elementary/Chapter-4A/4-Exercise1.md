# Exercise 1: Placing the Adverb of Frequency

Insira o advérbio de frequência indicado entre parênteses na posição gramaticalmente correta em cada uma das frases:

---

### Questões

a) She does my homework for me. *(sometimes)*  
b) Why are you late? *(always)*  
c) Does it rain in the summer? *(often)*  
d) They drive — they don't have a car. *(never)*  
e) I'm bored. *(hardly ever)*  

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

a) **She sometimes does my homework for me.**  
* *Justificativa:* Antes do verbo principal (*does*).

b) **Why are you always late?**  
* *Justificativa:* Após o verbo *to be* (*are*).

c) **Does it often rain in the summer?**  
* *Justificativa:* Em perguntas, entre o sujeito (*it*) e o verbo principal (*rain*).

d) **They never drive — they don't have a car.**  
* *Justificativa:* Antes do verbo principal (*drive*). Lembre-se: *never* não admite dupla negativa (*não use "don't never"*).

e) **I'm hardly ever bored.**  
* *Justificativa:* Após o verbo *to be* (*am / 'm*).
