📅 Adverbs of Frequency

Adverbs of frequency tell us how often something happens.

They usually answer the question:

How often...?

📊 Frequency Scale

| Frequency | Adverb                   | Example                                            |
| --------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **100%**  | **always**               | I always wake up at 7 o'clock.                     |
| **90%**   | **usually**              | I usually go home after I leave work.              |
| **80%**   | **normally / generally** | I normally check my emails before work.            |
| **70%**   | **often / frequently**   | I often visit my grandma at the weekend.           |
| **50%**   | **sometimes**            | I sometimes play rugby with my friends.            |
| **30%**   | **occasionally**         | I occasionally eat meat.                           |
| **15%**   | **seldom**               | I seldom watch TV.                                 |
| **5%**    | **rarely / hardly ever** | I rarely make grammar mistakes.                    |
| **0%**    | **never**                | I never forget a face. I have an excellent memory. |
