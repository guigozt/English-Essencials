📍 Position of Frequency Adverbs
✅ Before the main verb

Examples:

I always study at night.
She usually drinks coffee.
They often play football.
We never eat fast food.
✅ After the verb to be

Examples:

I am always tired after work.
She is usually happy.
They are never late.
He is often busy.

📌 Remember:

BE + Adverb

Not:

❌ I always am tired.

❌ Negative Sentences

The adverb goes between don't / doesn't and the main verb.

Examples:

I don't usually eat breakfast.
She doesn't often drive.
They don't normally work on Sundays.
❓ Questions

Adverbs are not usually placed in the question.

Instead, ask:

How often... ?

Examples:

How often do you study English?
How often does she visit her family?
How often do they travel?

📌 Frequency Adverbs × Frequency Expressions

| Frequency Adverbs | Frequency Expressions |
| ----------------- | --------------------- |
| always            | every day             |
| usually           | every week            |
| often             | every month           |
| sometimes         | every year            |
| rarely            | once a week           |
| never             | twice a week          |


Difference

Frequency adverbs describe frequency in a general way.

I usually study English.

Frequency expressions tell us the exact frequency.

I study English three times a week.

📖 Examples
Adverbs
I always wake up early.
She usually drinks tea.
We sometimes go to the beach.
They rarely eat fast food.
He never arrives late.

Expressions
I study English every day.
We visit our grandparents once a month.
She goes to the gym three times a week.
They travel every summer.
