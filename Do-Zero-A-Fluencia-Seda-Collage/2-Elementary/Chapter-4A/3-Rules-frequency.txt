Frequency adverbs and expressions of frquency

    1.  Frequency adverbs are used to:

    -   Go before all main verbs (except for the verb be):
        Ex: He 'never' smokes / I'm 'usually' late

    -   In negative sentences, the abverb goes between don't/ doesn't and the verb (except for ever / hardly ever):
        Ex: He doesn't 'always' play football on Mondays

    2.  Expressions of frequency are used to:

    -   Generally, go at the end sentences or verb phrases:
        Ex: I have German classes 'twice a week'
