Exercise 1 - Put the adverb in the correct place:

    a) She does my homework for me. (sometimes)
    b) Why are you late? (always)
    c) Does it rain in the summer? (often)
    d) They drive - They don't have a car. (never)
    e) I'm bored. (hardly ever)

Answers:

    1.  She sometimes does my homework for me. 
    2.  Why are you always late?
    3.  Does it often rain in the summer?
    4.  They never drive - They don't never have a car
    5.  I'm hardly ever bored
