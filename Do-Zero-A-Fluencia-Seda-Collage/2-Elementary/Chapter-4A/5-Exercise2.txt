Exercise 2 - Order the words to make sentences:

    a) our hardly teacher angry is ever
    b) for late never I am class
    c) you work every day to do drive?
    d) don't housework do we our always
    e) walk I every work to day

Answers:

    1.  Our teacher is hardly ever angry
    2.  I am never late for class
    3.  Do you drive to work every day?
    4.  We don't always do our housework
    5.  I walk to work every day
