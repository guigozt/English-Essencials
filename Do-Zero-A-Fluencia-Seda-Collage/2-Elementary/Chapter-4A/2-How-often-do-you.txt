🗓️ Expressions of Frequency

Expressions of frequency tell us exactly how often something happens.

They usually go at the end of the sentence.

| Expression         | Meaning               |
| ------------------ | --------------------- |
| every day          | todos os dias         |
| every week         | toda semana           |
| every month        | todo mês              |
| every year         | todo ano              |
| once a day         | uma vez por dia       |
| once a week        | uma vez por semana    |
| twice a week       | duas vezes por semana |
| three times a week | três vezes por semana |
| four times a month | quatro vezes por mês  |

💬 How Often Do You...?

| Question                               | Example Answer      |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| How often do you see your friends?     | Every day.          |
| How often do you go to the cinema?     | Every week.         |
| How often do you buy clothes?          | Every month.        |
| How often do you travel?               | Every year.         |
| How often do you talk to your grandma? | Once a week.        |
| How often do you take French classes?  | Twice a week.       |
| How often do you go to the gym?        | Three times a week. |
| How often do you eat out?              | Four times a week.  |
