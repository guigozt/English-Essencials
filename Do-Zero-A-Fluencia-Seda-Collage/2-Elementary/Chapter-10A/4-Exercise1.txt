Write the adjectives in the superlatives form:

    1.  bigger
    2.  drier
    3.  stronger
    4.  beautiful
    5.  handsome
    6.  black
    7.  blue
    8.  colder

Answers:

    1.  biggest
    2.  driest
    3.  strogest
    4.  the most beautiful
    5.  the most handsome
    6.  blackest
    7.  bluest
    8.  coldest
