Past Simple of To Be: Was / Were

We use was and were to talk about people, places, situations, and characteristics in the past.

Structure

| Subject | Verb |
| ------- | ---- |
| I       | was  |
| He      | was  |
| She     | was  |
| It      | was  |
| You     | were |
| We      | were |
| They    | were |

Examples
Martin Luther King Jr. was an activist.
Henry VIII was King of England.
The Beatles were famous in the 1960s.
Mary-Kate and Ashley were child actresses.

Affirmative

| Subject | Example           |
| ------- | ----------------- |
| I       | I was tired.      |
| He      | He was famous.    |
| She     | She was happy.    |
| It      | It was cold.      |
| You     | You were late.    |
| We      | We were students. |
| They    | They were famous. |


Negative

Add not.

| Full form | Short form |
| --------- | ---------- |
| was not   | wasn't     |
| were not  | weren't    |

Examples:

He wasn't at home.
She wasn't famous.
We weren't late.
They weren't students.

Questions

| Question          | Answer          |
| ----------------- | --------------- |
| Was he famous?    | Yes, he was.    |
| Was she at home?  | No, she wasn't. |
| Were they famous? | Yes, they were. |
| Were you tired?   | No, I wasn't.   |

Structure

Was/Were + subject + complement?

Examples:

Was Martin Luther King Jr. an activist?
Were the Olsen twins born in California?

Time Expressions

We often use was/were with expressions that refer to the past.

| Expression   | Example                               |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| yesterday    | I was tired yesterday.                |
| last night   | He was at home last night.            |
| last week    | We were busy last week.               |
| in 1985      | She was born in 1985.                 |
| in the 1960s | The Beatles were famous in the 1960s. |

Was/Were + Born

Essa é uma estrutura fixa.

| Singular     | Plural         |
| ------------ | -------------- |
| I was born   | We were born   |
| He was born  | They were born |
| She was born | You were born  |

Examples:

I was born in Brazil.
She was born in Australia.
They were born in 1986.
