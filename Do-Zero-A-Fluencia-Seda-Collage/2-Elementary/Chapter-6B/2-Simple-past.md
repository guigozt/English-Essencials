# Past Simple of To Be: Was & Were

Utilizamos **was** e **were** para descrever pessoas, lugares, estados emocionais, condições climáticas e características no passado.

---

### 1. Conjugação Básica

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{I\ /\ He\ /\ She\ /\ It} &\longrightarrow \mathbf{was} \\
\mathbf{You\ /\ We\ /\ They} &\longrightarrow \mathbf{were}
\end{aligned}$$

---

### 2. Formas Afirmativa, Negativa e Interrogativa

| Sujeito | Afirmativa $(+)$ | Negativa $(-)$ | Pergunta $(?)$ | Respostas Curtas (*Short Answers*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I** | I **was** tired. | I **wasn't** tired. | **Was** I late? | *Yes, you were. / No, you weren't.* |
| **He** | He **was** famous. | He **wasn't** famous. | **Was** he famous? | *Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't.* |
| **She** | She **was** happy. | She **wasn't** happy. | **Was** she at home? | *Yes, she was. / No, she wasn't.* |
| **It** | It **was** cold. | It **wasn't** cold. | **Was** it sunny? | *Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.* |
| **You** | You **were** late. | You **weren't** late. | **Were** you tired? | *Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.* |
| **We** | We **were** students. | We **weren't** students. | **Were** we right? | *Yes, we were. / No, we weren't.* |
| **They** | They **were** actors. | They **weren't** actors. | **Were** they ready? | *Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.* |

* Contrações:
  * *was not* $\rightarrow$ **wasn't**
  * *were not* $\rightarrow$ **weren't**

---

### 3. A Estrutura Fixa: Was / Were + Born (Nascer)

Em inglês, o ato de nascer é sempre expresso com o passado do verbo *to be* + o particípio *born*:

| Pessoa | Estrutura | Exemplo Prático |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Singular** | *I / He / She / It* **was born** | • *I was born in Brazil in 1995.*<br>• *She was born in London.* |
| **Plural** | *You / We / They* **were born** | • *They were born in California.*<br>• *Where were you born?* |

---

### 4. Expressões de Tempo Típicas com Was / Were

* **yesterday:** *I was tired yesterday.* (Eu estava cansado ontem.)
* **last night:** *He was at home last night.* (Ele estava em casa ontem à noite.)
* **last week / month / year:** *We were busy last week.* (Estávamos ocupados na semana passada.)
* **in 1986 / in the 1960s:** *The Beatles were famous in the 1960s.* (Os Beatles eram famosos nos anos 60.)
