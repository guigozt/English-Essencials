# Exercise 1: Was or Were?

Selecione a opção correta entre parênteses para completar cada oração de acordo com o sujeito e a estrutura de tempo passado:

---

### Questões

a) She **(were / was)** born in 1850.  
b) What **(was / were)** his name again?  
c) They **(was / were)** at home yesterday.  
d) **(Were / Was)** she at school this morning?  
e) You **(were / was)** an excellent sportsman.  

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Justificativas</b></summary>

<br>

a) She **was** born in 1850.  
* *Justificativa:* Sujeito de 3ª pessoa do singular (*she*) concorda com **was**.

b) What **was** his name again?  
* *Justificativa:* O sujeito é "his name" (singular / equivalente a *it*), logo usa-se **was**.

c) They **were** at home yesterday.  
* *Justificativa:* Sujeito plural de 3ª pessoa (*they*) concorda com **were**.

d) **Was** she at school this morning?  
* *Justificativa:* Pergunta com sujeito singular (*she*) inicia-se com **Was**.

e) You **were** an excellent sportsman.  
* *Justificativa:* O pronome *you* (seja singular ou plural) sempre concorda com **were**.
