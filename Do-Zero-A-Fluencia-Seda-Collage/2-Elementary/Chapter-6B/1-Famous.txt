Famous People – Describing People in the Past

Quando descrevemos pessoas históricas ou falamos sobre suas vidas, usamos principalmente o Simple Past, porque estamos falando de acontecimentos e características do passado.

1. Martin Luther King Jr.
Corrected text

This photograph, taken by a famous photographer from New York, shows Martin Luther King Jr., who was a civil rights activist during the 1950s and 1960s.

Martin Luther King Jr. was famous for leading non-violent protests to fight for equal rights for everyone, especially African Americans.

Vocabulary

| Word         | Meaning         |
| ------------ | --------------- |
| photograph   | fotografia      |
| taken by     | tirada por      |
| civil rights | direitos civis  |
| activist     | ativista        |
| non-violent  | não violento    |
| protest      | protesto        |
| equal rights | direitos iguais |

2. Henry VIII

Corrected text

This painting, by an unknown painter, portrays Henry VIII, who was King of England during the first half of the 16th century.

He is famous for separating the Church of England from the Roman Catholic Church and for having six wives.

When he was young, he wasn't very good-looking, but he was quite muscular.

Henry was a talented musician and poet, and he was also a great sportsman.

| Word            | Meaning                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------- |
| painting        | pintura                   |
| unknown painter | pintor desconhecido       |
| portray         | retratar                  |
| good-looking    | bonito / de boa aparência |
| muscular        | musculoso                 |
| talented        | talentoso                 |
| poet            | poeta                     |
| sportsman       | esportista                |


3. Mary-Kate and Ashley Olsen
Corrected text

This is a colour photograph of Mary-Kate Olsen and her twin sister, Ashley Olsen.

The Olsen twins were born in 1986 in California.

They became famous as child actresses after appearing in the sitcom Full House when they were only nine months old.

They were a huge hit in the 1980s and 1990s.

Nowadays, the Olsen twins work as creative directors in the fashion industry.
