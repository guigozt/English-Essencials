Select the correct option:

    a) She were / was born in 1850.
    b) What was / were his name again?
    c) They was / were at home yesterday.
    d) Were / Was she at school this morning?
    e) You were / was an excellent sportsman.

Answer:

    1.  She was born in 1850
    2.  What was his name again?
    3.  They were at home yesterday
    4.  Was she at school this morning?
    5.  You were an excellent sportsman
