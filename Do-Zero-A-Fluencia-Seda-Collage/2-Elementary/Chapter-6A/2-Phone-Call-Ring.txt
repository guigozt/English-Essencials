📱 Phone vs Call vs Ring

These three can all mean "ligar", but there are some differences.

Phone

Very common in British English.

I phoned my father yesterday.

I'll phone you tonight.


Call

Very common in both British and American English.

I'll call you later.

She called me this morning.


Give someone a ring

Informal expression, especially common in British English.

I'll give you a ring tonight.

It means exactly:

"I'll call you."

📌 You don't literally give someone a physical ring. 😄


📵 Can / Can't Reach

Reach someone means manage to contact or speak to someone.

Positive

I can reach my father.

= Consigo falar com meu pai.

Negative

I can't reach my father.

= Não consigo falar com meu pai.

Examples:

I tried calling Sarah, but I couldn't reach her.
Can you reach John?
I can't reach my boss at the moment.
She's not answering her phone, so I can't reach her.

🔙 Call Someone Back

Call someone back = retornar a ligação / ligar de volta.

Examples:

I'll call you back later.

She called me, but I was busy, so I called her back after work.

Notice the word order

Call + person + back

I'll call you back.

She called me back.

You can also say:

I'll call back later.

when you don't mention the person.

📞 Put Someone on the Phone

Put someone on the phone means passar o telefone para alguém so that the other person can speak to them.

Example:

Can you put your mother on the phone, please?

= Você pode passar o telefone para sua mãe?

Another example:

Dad wants to talk to you. I'll put him on the phone.

🎥 Make a Video Call

Make a video call = fazer uma chamada de vídeo.

Examples:

I'm making a video call to my sister.
We usually make video calls on Sundays.
She is making a video call with her parents.

📶 Connection
A good connection

We have a good connection.

= A conexão está boa.

A bad connection

We have a bad connection.

= A conexão está ruim.

Examples:

Sorry, I can't hear you. The connection is bad.

Can you hear me? The connection is terrible.

You're breaking up.

= Sua voz está cortando.

💡 You're breaking up é uma expressão muito útil quando a ligação está falhando.

📞 Answer the Phone

Answer the phone = atender o telefone.

Examples:

Can you answer the phone?

She didn't answer the phone.

Why didn't you answer my call?

📌 Também podemos dizer:

answer a call

He didn't answer my call.
