📞 Phone Language

English has several different ways to talk about making and receiving phone calls.

📱 Example 1 – Talking on the Phone

This is a photo of me phoning my father.

He rarely calls me during the week, so I generally give him a ring every Monday morning to see if everything is OK.

My mother, on the other hand, phones me on a daily basis. I'm usually busy during the day, but I always call her back when I arrive home.

They adopted a new cat and called it Garlic. Dad is so funny! Whenever I can reach him, he puts the cat on the phone!

📖 Vocabulary & Expressions

| Expression                   | Meaning                    | Example                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **phone someone**            | ligar para alguém          | I phoned my father.                                            |
| **call someone**             | ligar para alguém          | She called me yesterday.                                       |
| **give someone a ring**      | ligar para alguém          | I'll give you a ring later.                                    |
| **reach someone**            | conseguir falar com alguém | I can't reach John.                                            |
| **call someone back**        | retornar a ligação         | I'll call you back later.                                      |
| **put someone on the phone** | passar alguém ao telefone  | Can you put your mother on the phone?                          |
| **on the other hand**        | por outro lado             | My dad is quiet. My mom, on the other hand, is very talkative. |
| **on a daily basis**         | diariamente                | She phones me on a daily basis.                                |
| **during the week**          | durante a semana           | I'm usually busy during the week.                              |
| **whenever**                 | sempre que                 | Whenever I call him, he's busy.                                |
