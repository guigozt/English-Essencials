Select the correct option:

    a) Jenny meets him / he after work every day.
    b) Do you really love I / me?
    c) I can't talk to she / her right now. I'm sorry.
    d) She / Her is the best student in our class.
    e) My friends and I / me are travelling during the week.

Answer:

    1.  Jenny meets him after work every day
    2.  Do you really love me?
    3.  I can't talk to her right now. I'm sorry
    4.  She is the best student in our classes
    5.  My friends and I are travelling during the week
