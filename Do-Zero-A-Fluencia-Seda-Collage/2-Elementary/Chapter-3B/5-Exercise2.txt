Complete with to, at, in or -

    a) Let's go ______ the beach!
    b) She studies Law ______ the University of New York.
    c) We live ______ a flat.
    d) What time do you go _______ home?
    e) Joana's boyfriend works _______ an office.

Answer

    1.  Let's go to the beach
    2.  She studies Law at the University of New York
    3.  We live in a flat
    4.  What time do you go at home?
    5.  Joana's boyfriend works in a office
