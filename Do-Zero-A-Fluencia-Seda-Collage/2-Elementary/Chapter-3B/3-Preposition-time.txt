📅 Prepositions of Time

Prepositions of time tell us when something happens.

| **in**           | **on**                 | **at**         |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- | -------------- |
| in the morning   | on Monday              | at 3 o'clock   |
| in the afternoon | on Friday              | at midday      |
| in the evening   | on my birthday         | at midnight    |
| in January       | on Christmas Day       | at lunchtime   |
| in July          | on weekends *(AmE)*    | at Christmas   |
| in 2026          | on the weekend *(AmE)* | at Easter      |
| in summer        | on Sunday morning      | **at night** ⭐ |

📌 When do we use them?
🟦 In

Use in for:

Parts of the day
Months
Years
Seasons
Long periods of time

Examples:

in the morning
in July
in 2026
in summer

🟩 On

Use on for:

Days
Dates
Specific days + parts of the day

Examples:

on Monday
on my birthday
on Sunday morning
on Christmas Day

💡 Note: Quando você especifica o dia, usa on:

in the morning ✅
on Monday morning ✅
on Sunday afternoon ✅

🟥 At

Use at for:

Exact times
Holidays
Specific moments
Night

Examples:

at 7 o'clock
at midday
at lunchtime
at Christmas
at night
