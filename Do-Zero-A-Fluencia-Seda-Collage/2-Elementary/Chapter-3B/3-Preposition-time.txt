Prepositions of time

    | **in**           | **on**                 | **at**         |
    | ---------------- | ---------------------- | -------------- |
    | in the morning   | on Monday              | at 3 o'clock   |
    | in the afternoon | on Tuesday             | at midday      |
    | in the evening   | on Wednesday           | at midnight    |
    | in January       | on Friday              | at lunchtime   |
    | in 2026          | on weekends *(AmE)*    | at Christmas   |
    | in summer        | on the weekend *(AmE)* | at Easter      |
    | in July          | on my birthday         | **at night** ⭐ |


    -   We use "in" for parts of the day and seasons
    -   We use "on" for days
    -   We use "at" for times of the days and festivals
