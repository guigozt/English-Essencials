Prepositions of time

        in          |       on      |    at
    -----------------------------------------------
    the morning     |   Monday      |   three o'clock
    the afternoon   |   Tuesday     |   midday / midnight
    the evening     |   Wednesday   |   lunchtime  
    the summer      |   Wekeends    |   Christmas

    -   We use "in" for parts of the day and seasons
    -   We use "on" for days
    -   We use "at" for times of the days and festivals
