📍 Prepositions of Place

Prepositions of place tell us where someone or something is.

| **in**          | **to**                 | **at**          |
| --------------- | ---------------------- | --------------- |
| in a flat       | go to work             | at work         |
| in a house      | go to school           | at school       |
| in a restaurant | go to university       | at university   |
| in an office    | get back to the office | at home         |
| in a room       | go to the cinema       | at the cinema   |
| in the kitchen  | go to the market       | at the bus stop |

📌 When do we use them?
🟦 In

Use in when something is inside a place.

Examples:

She is in the kitchen.
We live in a flat.
He works in an office.
They are in the restaurant.

🟩 To

Use to for movement or direction.

Examples:

I go to work.
She walks to school.
They travel to Dublin.
We go to the cinema.

🟥 At

Use at for a specific location or point.

Examples:

I'm at home.
She's at work.
They're at school.
We met at the restaurant.
