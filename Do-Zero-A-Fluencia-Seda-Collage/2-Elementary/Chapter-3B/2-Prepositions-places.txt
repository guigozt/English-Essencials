Prepositions of Place

        in          |   to          |   at
    -----------------------------------------------
    a flat          |   get back... |   work
    a house         |   go...       |   home
    a restaurant    |               |   school 
    an office       |               |   university

    -   We use "to" for movement and direction
    -   We use "at" and "in" for position
    -   We use "at" + work, home, school, cinema
    -   We use "in" + other places: a flat, an office, a room
