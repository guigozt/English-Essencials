# Parts of the House (Cômodos e Áreas da Casa)

Guia visual e referencial dos cômodos e partes de uma casa ou prédio residencial com suas respectivas funções cotidianas.

---

### 🏡 Tabela de Cômodos & Exemplos de Uso

| Cômodo em Inglês | Tradução em Português | O que fazemos lá? (*Action / Example*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **building** | prédio / edifício | *They live in a tall modern building.* |
| **bedroom** | quarto | *She is sleeping in the bedroom.* |
| **living room** | sala de estar | *We watch TV and relax in the living room.* |
| **dining room** | sala de jantar | *They are having dinner in the dining room.* |
| **kitchen** | cozinha | *My mother is cooking lunch in the kitchen.* |
| **bathroom** | banheiro | *He is taking a hot shower in the bathroom.* |
| **laundry room** | lavanderia / área de serviço | *She is doing the laundry in the laundry room.* |
| **basement** | porão / subsolo | *The bicycles and tools are stored in the basement.* |
| **attic** | sótão | *Old boxes and books are kept in the attic.* |
| **hall / hallway** | corredor / hall de entrada | *Leave your shoes in the hall.* |
| **garage** | garagem | *Park the car inside the garage.* |
| **garden / backyard** | jardim / quintal | *The children love playing in the garden.* |
| **balcony** | varanda / sacada | *We enjoy morning coffee on the balcony.* |
