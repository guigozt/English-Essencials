Present Continuous: be + verb + ing

    We use the present continuous for:

    1.  Things that are happening at the moment: 
        -   My dad is talking on the phone now

    2.  Longer periods of time (today, this week...):
        -   I'm working at home this week
        

            +           |           -               |           ?
    -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    I'm having a party  |   I'm not having a party  |   Are you having a party
