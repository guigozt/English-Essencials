📚 Present Continuous
Structure

Subject + am / is / are + Verb + ing

| Subject | Verb "to be" | Verb + ing | Example           |
| ------- | ------------ | ---------- | ----------------- |
| I       | am           | working    | I am working.     |
| You     | are          | studying   | You are studying. |
| He      | is           | sleeping   | He is sleeping.   |
| She     | is           | cooking    | She is cooking.   |
| It      | is           | raining    | It is raining.    |
| We      | are          | eating     | We are eating.    |
| They    | are          | playing    | They are playing. |

When do we use it?
1️⃣ Actions happening now

Use it for actions happening at this moment.

Examples:

My dad is talking on the phone now.
She is reading a book.
They are watching TV.
We are studying English.
2️⃣ Temporary situations

Use it for situations happening around the present time, but not necessarily at this exact moment.

Examples:

I'm working from home this week.
She is staying with her grandparents this month.
We are learning about the Present Continuous today.

Sentence Structure

| Positive            | Negative                | Question                |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| I'm having a party. | I'm not having a party. | Are you having a party? |
| She is cooking.     | She isn't cooking.      | Is she cooking?         |
| They are studying.  | They aren't studying.   | Are they studying?      |

Short Answers

| Question          | Positive       | Negative         |
| ----------------- | -------------- | ---------------- |
| Are you studying? | Yes, I am.     | No, I'm not.     |
| Is she sleeping?  | Yes, she is.   | No, she isn't.   |
| Are they working? | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |
