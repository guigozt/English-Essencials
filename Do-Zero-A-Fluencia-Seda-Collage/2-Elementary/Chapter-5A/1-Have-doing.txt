🏢 Parts of the House & Present Continuous
📖 Look at this building

Question:

What are the residents doing?

👉 We use the Present Continuous to describe what people are doing right now.

🏠 Describing the Residents
👨‍🍳 Jack and Rose

Jack and Rose are having a good time. They are cooking in the kitchen.

📌 Vocabulary

have a good time = divertir-se
cook = cozinhar
kitchen = cozinha

🧹 Julia, Joska and Jenny

Julia, Joska and Jenny are cleaning the living room. That's great teamwork!

📌 Vocabulary

clean = limpar
living room = sala de estar
teamwork = trabalho em equipe

📺😴 George

George was watching TV earlier, but now he is sleeping in the bedroom.

📌 Vocabulary

earlier = mais cedo
watch TV = assistir TV
sleep = dormir
bedroom = quarto

📌 Notice:

Past → Present

Earlier → was watching
Now → is sleeping

🍽️ Paul and Linda

Paul and Linda are having a romantic dinner. They are eating in the dining room.

📌 Vocabulary

romantic dinner = jantar romântico
dining room = sala de jantar

🚿 Dave

Dave is in the bathroom. He is taking a shower after a long day at work.

📌 Vocabulary

take a shower = tomar banho
after work = depois do trabalho

👕

Rita is in the laundry room right now. She doesn't have much time during the week, so she is doing the laundry today.

📌 Vocabulary

laundry room = lavanderia
do the laundry = lavar roupas
during the week = durante a semana

💡 Note:

Em inglês, o mais comum é dizer laundry room, em vez de apenas laundry, quando estamos falando do cômodo da casa.

🚲 Sean

Sean is in the basement, looking for his blue bike. It's really dark downstairs.

📌 Vocabulary

basement = porão
look for = procurar
downstairs = andar de baixo

💡 É mais natural dizer looking for do que searching for nesse contexto.
