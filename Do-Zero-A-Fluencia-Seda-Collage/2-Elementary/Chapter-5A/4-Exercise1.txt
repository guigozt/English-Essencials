Complete the sentences using parts of the house:

    a) We wash our clothes in the _____ .
    b) We take a shower in the _____ .
    c) We store old things in the _____ .
    d) We have lunch in the _____ .
    e) We watch TV in the _____ .

Answers:

    1.  We wash our clothes in the laundry
    2.  We take a shower in the bathroom
    3.  We store old thing in the basement
    4.  We have lunch in the dining room
    5.  We watch TV in the livinh room
