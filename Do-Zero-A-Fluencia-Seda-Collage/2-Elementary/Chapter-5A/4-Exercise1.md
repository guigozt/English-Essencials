# Exercise 1: Parts of the House

Complete cada frase com o cômodo ou área da casa mais apropriado:

---

### Questões

a) We wash our clothes in the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ .  
b) We take a shower in the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ .  
c) We store old things in the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ .  
d) We have lunch and dinner in the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ .  
e) We watch TV and relax in the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ .  

---

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Ver Respostas e Comentários</b></summary>

<br>

a) We wash our clothes in the **laundry room** *(ou laundry)*.  
b) We take a shower in the **bathroom**.  
c) We store old things in the **basement** *(ou attic)*.  
d) We have lunch in the **dining room** *(ou kitchen)*.  
e) We watch TV in the **living room**.  

</details>
